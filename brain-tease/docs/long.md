# BRAIN TEASE — Trap Puzzle Lab

The Doctor has built 24 absurd contraptions, and every "obvious" answer is a trap. BRAIN TEASE is a tricky-puzzle game where you tap, drag and flip your way through pranks: cool a thermometer by dragging it into ice water, turn on a bulb by clicking its shadow, drag a poster off the wall to find the real button, and flip the whole scene upside down when gravity disagrees with you.

## How to play

- **Tap** an object to try it.
- **Drag** objects onto each other — or drag covers, blankets and posters out of the way.
- **Flip** the scene with the rotate handle when a level demands it.
- Stuck? Tap **Hint** (costs one star this level). Wrong three times and the **Doctor helps** auto-solves the level for one star.
- Every 6 levels is a multi-step **Boss** puzzle. 24 levels, 4 scene packs (Lab, Kitchen, Garden, Space).
- Race your own **local best time** and earn up to 3 stars per level.

Playable on desktop (mouse) and mobile (touch, landscape). Rotate your phone for the best view.

## 脑力试炼 — 整蛊实验室

博士发明了 24 个整蛊装置，每个「显而易见」的答案都是陷阱。这是一款反直觉解谜游戏：把温度计拖进冰水降温、连点灯泡但第三次要点影子、掀开海报找真按钮、倒置场景让沙漏倒流……点错了博士会坏笑，点对了博士会魔性吐槽。

## 怎么玩

- **点击**物体尝试交互。
- **拖拽**物体：把温度计拖进冰水、把锅拖进水池，或拖走遮挡物（海报 / 毯子 / 保护罩）。
- 遇到需要倒置的关卡，**拖旋转把手**翻转场景。
- 卡关点「提示」会扣一颗星；答错 3 次解锁「博士帮你」半自动通关（只 1 星）。
- 每 6 关一个多步 **Boss** 谜题。共 24 关、4 套场景（实验室 / 厨房 / 花园 / 太空控制室）。
- 冲击你自己的**本地最佳时间**，每关最多 3 星。

支持桌面鼠标与移动端触屏（横屏体验最佳，可旋转设备获得最佳视角）。
