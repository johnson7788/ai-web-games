# Blind Box Memory — Flip, Match & Collect!

Blind Box Memory is a cozy memory-match puzzle packed with collectible blind-box characters. Every card hides a surprise figurine — flip two at a time, find the twins, and clear the table before your moves run out.

## How to play

- Tap any card to flip it, then flip another to find its matching twin.
- Every pair of flips costs 1 move — run out of moves and the level fails.
- Chain matches without a miss to build combos and earn bonus coins.
- Stuck? Use a Peek to briefly reveal a matching pair.

## What makes it special

- 31 hand-picked collectible characters to discover — every matched pair joins your Collection gallery.
- 12 levels that keep adding one new twist at a time:
  - Star pairs that pay triple coins,
  - Trap cards that never match,
  - Mirrored twins you can't memorize pixel-by-pixel,
  - Same-family decoy characters that test your eye for detail,
  - Combo chains that refund moves,
  - Phantom shuffles that rearrange the table,
  - Flash reveals that test your speed memory,
  - Four-of-a-kind cards where any two twins match.
- 3-star rating on every level: finish with moves to spare for maximum stars and coins.
- Spend coins in the Shop on permanent upgrades — extra moves, combo shields, precise peeks and more.
- Fully playable offline — smooth 60fps card flipping with juicy 3D flip animations and synthesized sound effects.

Play in English or Chinese, on desktop or mobile. No downloads, no accounts — just flip, match and collect!
