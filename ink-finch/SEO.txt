SHORT DESCRIPTION

LONG DESCRIPTION
INK FINCH is a free one-tap arcade game about a little ink bird flitting through a bamboo sea.

Tap, press Space, or touch anywhere to flap. Every beat of the wing splatters a dot of ink — your signature in the world. Thread the gap in each bamboo stalk to score a vermilion point. Clip a stalk, the ground or the sky, and the ink spills — one tap restarts you instantly.

12 handcrafted levels across three ink-wash worlds: Morning Mist, Rainy Night and Snowy Path. Narrow gaps test your precision, swaying gaps test your timing, updrafts let you glide, headwinds push you sideways, twin gaps offer a risky +3 or a safe +1, and spinning spike wheels guard the later paths. A final master stage stacks them all.

Black, white and grey ink with a single touch of vermilion. Easy to learn in ten seconds, hard to put down. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download — play free in your browser.

点击、按空格或触屏任意处即可上扑。每一次振翅都溅出一滴墨点——那是你在世界里的签名。穿过每根竹节的缝口，记一分朱砂红；撞竹、落地或触顶，墨就洒了——再点一下，立刻重开。
