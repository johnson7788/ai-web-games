# OCEAN DASH

Lead a migrating school of fish in a side-scrolling race against three rival schools. Your fish swims forward automatically — steer up and down to slip past reefs, jellyfish, and mangrove roots. Eat bubbles to heal and sprint; ride glowing current bands to snap your school into a speeding arrow of light. The signature choice is **scatter vs gather**: hold to tighten the school and squeeze through narrow gaps (but slow down), or release to fan out for speed (but present a bigger target). Leap out of the water and glide across shallow reef shortcuts to pull ahead.

Race through **12 segments** across three original scene packs — bright-cyan Coral Shallows, deep-blue Deep Trench, and green-gold Mangrove — each introducing a new hazard or trick. Finish rank 2 or better to advance; lose your whole school and the race is over. Chase new best-segment records, near-misses, and the perfect current-chain run.

**Controls**
- ↑ / W / ↓ / S — steer up and down (or drag / hover the pointer)
- Hold Space — gather the school (narrow but slow)
- J (or tap/click) — jump out of the water; hold while airborne to glide
- Esc / P — pause · R — race again

OCEAN DASH 是一款侧视竞速网页游戏：你带领一群洄游的鱼，自动向前游动，上下躲避礁石、水母与红树根。吃气泡回血加速，借洋流带让鱼群瞬间列成箭形光带冲向终点。核心决定是**散开 vs 收拢**——按住收拢能穿过窄缝但变慢，松开散开更快但更容易撞上障碍；跃出水面滑翔可跨过礁石捷径。12 个赛段、三套原创场景包（珊瑚浅滩 / 深沟 / 红树林），每段引入一个新机制，名次 ≤2 才能晋级。
