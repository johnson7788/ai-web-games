SHORT DESCRIPTION

LONG DESCRIPTION
SHADOW NINJA CAT is a free side-scrolling action runner about a ninja cat racing across moonlit rooftops at night.

The rooftops never stop moving. Tap, press Space or swipe to jump over gaps and spiked eaves, then press Down to dive — stay low to slip under enemy shurikens. Every run is a single breathless dash: collect glowing wind chimes, spend them in the Shadow Shop on a double-jump cloak, a grappling claw, a guard bell, or single-use charms like the bamboo copter, bell magnet and echo bell.

12 levels across three hand-painted night scenes: the Dusk Tile Sea, the Rainy Night Alley, and the Moonlit Bamboo Forest. Each level adds a new twist — spiked eaves, shuriken volleys, a chasing dog that forces you faster, high and low lanes, wind-chime chains, night fog, a rooftop zip-line, and the final Daimyo Castle gauntlet.

Three stars per level: reach the gate, hit the wind-chime goal, and finish without a scratch. Seven ninja missions track your lifetime chimes, distance and prop mastery. Easy to pick up in ten seconds, hard to put down.

Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download — play free in your browser.
