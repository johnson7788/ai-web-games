# STACK SKY — Cloud Island Tower

**Stack floating islands into the sky.** A floating island slides left and right above your tower. Tap (or press Space) to drop it. If it lines up perfectly with the island below, you get a **Perfect** combo and your tower grows wider again. If it's off, the overhang is cut away and the next island gets narrower. Stack higher to climb through three realms: the golden **Dawn Clouds**, the blue-gray **Storm Layer**, and the deep-purple **Starry Palace**.

Two twists keep every climb tense:

- **Wind shear** — every 10 layers a crosswind pushes the moving island sideways. Anticipate the drift or lose width.
- **Shrine layers** — every 15 layers, aligning the shrine pattern perfectly restores your island to full width.

Chain **Perfect ×3** to trigger the signature **Cloud Wrap**: your whole tower glows gold and the sea of clouds surges.

## How to play

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Drop the island | Click / tap / Space |
| Pause | Esc or P |
| Restart (after falling) | R |
| Start | Enter (title) |

Works with mouse, keyboard, and touch. Rotate to landscape on mobile for the best view.

# STACK SKY · 云端叠塔

**把浮空岛一层层叠上云霄。** 一块浮空岛在塔顶上方左右往复滑动，点击（或按空格）截停它：与下方岛屿完全对齐就是 **Perfect** 连击、塔身还会变宽一点；没对齐的部分会被裁掉，下一块变得更窄。叠得越高，你越会穿过三片天域——金白的**晨曦云海**、蓝灰的**雷雨层**、深紫的**星夜天宫**。

两个原创机制让每次攀登都绷紧神经：

- **风切**——每 10 层会刮来一阵横风，把移动岛吹偏，需要预判提前截停。
- **神龛层**——每 15 层有一次机会对齐神龛纹样，成功即恢复初始宽度。

连续 **Perfect×3** 触发签名时刻「**彩云缠绕**」：整座塔泛起金光，背景云海翻涌。

## 操作

| 动作 | 操作 |
| --- | --- |
| 截停浮空岛 | 点击 / 触摸 / 空格 |
| 暂停 | Esc 或 P |
| 失败后重开 | R |
| 标题屏开始 | Enter |

支持鼠标、键盘、触屏；手机请横屏获得最佳体验。
