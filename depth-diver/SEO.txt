DEPTH DIVER is a free deep-sea arcade game about piloting a research submersible into a Mariana-style trench where depth is your score and darkness is your enemy.

Hold to rise, release to sink, and nudge left and right to thread through rocks, mines, hydrothermal vents and a chasing anglerfish. Your headlight carves a cone of light through the dark, and every new depth layer sweeps the black away while a swarm of bioluminescent life scatters in front of you.

Descend through three worlds: the Sunlit Shallows, the Twilight Zone and the Midnight Abyss. Each level adds a new rule — mine fields, drifting currents, rising vents, a pursuing angler, narrow crevices, shrinking light, crushing pressure, and the final abyss where everything stacks against you.
