**ABYSS EEL** is a free deep-sea arcade game that runs in your browser — dive as a glowing eel made of light orbs, smash numbered coral walls, and chase your own depth record into the dark.

You are a bioluminescent eel falling through five ocean zones, from sunlit water to hydrothermal vents. Your body is a chain of glowing orbs — it is your health, your ammo, **and your light**: the fewer orbs you carry, the darker the abyss gets.

### How it plays
- **Steer** — drag with mouse or finger (or A/D, arrow keys). The eel bends and follows.
- **Break walls** — every coral block has a number. Hitting it costs one orb per point; clearing it bursts into sparks that fly back to feed you.
- **Dash** — hold Space / Shift / the dash button to burn orbs fast and smash walls at double speed. Risk your length for combo glory.
- **Chain combos** — break walls within 2.5s of each other to build a multiplier up to ×3. Getting hit breaks the chain.
- **Survive the zones** — moving walls in the Twilight, darkness and golden orbs in the Midnight, sea-urchin mines in the Abyss, and a regenerating five-segment Boss wall at every 900m past the vents.

### Why it's different
- **Light is life**: orbs double as vision — losing health literally narrows your world.
- **The signature moment**: hold dash and the whole eel becomes a blue-white beam punching through a coral wall, sparks vacuuming back into your chain.
- **Every wall is arithmetic with a price**: go around, go through, or burn your own light.

Free to play, no download. Works on desktop and mobile. English and Chinese.
