# DUNE BUGGY

**DUNE BUGGY** is a Baja desert rally arcade. Drive a dune buggy across 12 hand-tuned rally stages, from golden dawn sands to the white heat of noon and the orange-purple dusk. Every stage is a race against the clock — beat the target time to advance, or run out of time and DNF.

The one rule that changes everything is the **golden sand ridge**: a glowing crest runs along parts of the track. Ride it to keep a constant speed boost, but drift off and you sink into soft sand. The second rule is your **tire pressure**: high pressure is fast but slides on sand; low pressure is slower but grips. You pick before each stage and switch with **T** — a pit-stop tradeoff, not a stat you set once.

Terrain rotates as you go: soft sand, rock fields that kill your grip, oasis jump ramps that launch the camera up to spot your landing, and blinding sandstorms that cut visibility. Three scene packs change the whole palette and silhouette so you always know which chapter you're in.

**Controls**

- **↑ / W** — throttle
- **↓ / S** — brake
- **← → / A D** — steer
- **T** — switch tire pressure (high = fast/slippery, low = stable/slower)
- **Esc / P** — pause · **R** — retry · **Enter** — start
- Touch: on-screen buttons for steering, gas, brake and tire pressure.

**DUNE BUGGY** 是一款巴哈沙丘拉力街机游戏。开着沙丘越野车，从金色晨沙、白热午漠一路开到橙紫暮丘，闯过 12 个拉力赛段，每个赛段都要抢在目标时间内冲线，否则就会超时退赛。

贯穿全程的核心规则是**金色沙脊**：赛道上一道发光的沙脊，骑上去能持续加速，偏离就会陷沙减速。第二条规则是**胎压**：高胎压极速更快但沙地打滑，低胎压更稳但更慢——每次开赛前、每段换地形时用 **T** 切换，这是一次有代价的取舍。

地形不断轮换：软沙、岩区、绿洲跳台（飞跃时相机拉升俯瞰落点）、沙暴区（能见度骤降），三套场景包换色板、换构图、换装饰，章节一目了然。

**操作**

- **↑ / W** 油门 · **↓ / S** 刹车 · **← → / A D** 转向 · **T** 切换胎压
- **Esc / P** 暂停 · **R** 重开 · **Enter** 开始
- 触屏：屏幕下方提供转向、油门、刹车与胎压按钮
