# BALL ORBIT — Slingshot Star Sea

Fire strings of meteors from a bottom slingshot into a descending asteroid belt. Every hit shaves one point of an asteroid's hardness; clear it to zero and it shatters. Between rounds the whole belt sinks one row — if it reaches the bottom, the belt overruns you.

The star field fights back: **planets bend your trajectories with real gravity**, so line up a shot that loops around a planet's far side to crack high-value asteroids from behind. Clearing 50 hardness charges a **comet** with ×3 impact; save it for shielded asteroids that shrug off normal meteors. Clearing an entire row triggers a **gravity ripple** that warps the neighboring rows.

- **12 waves** across three scene packs: Inner Dust Belt (warm gray) → Gas Giant (teal) → Deep Space (blue-violet), each introducing new threats (multi-planet fields, drifting meteors, shield meteors, bounce-double bands).
- **Controls**: drag anywhere to aim (the dotted line previews the gravity-bent path), release to fire the whole string. Esc pauses, R restarts after defeat.

# BALL ORBIT · 弹弓星海

从底部弹弓发射一串流星，击碎不断下坠的陨石带。每撞一次，陨石硬度 -1；归零即碎裂。每回合结束后，整条陨石带下坠一行——一旦触底，游戏结束。

星空会反击：**行星用真实引力弯曲你的弹道**，绕到行星背面就能打穿高值陨石。每清 50 硬度蓄一颗**彗星**（撞击力 ×3），留着砸碎普通流星打不动的**护盾陨石**。清空整行会触发**引力涟漪**，把邻行陨石吸得歪斜。

- **12 波**，三场景包：内环尘带（暖灰）→ 气态巨星（青绿）→ 深空（蓝紫），每波引入新威胁（多行星引力、移动陨石、护盾陨石、弹射加倍带）。
- **操作**：任意位置拖动瞄准（虚线预览引力弯曲后的弹道），松开发射整串流星；Esc 暂停，失败后按 R 重开。
