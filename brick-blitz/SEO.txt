SHORT DESCRIPTION
Fire a red-hot clay bead and smash the glaze-brick wall across three kilns in this free breakout-style arcade game.

LONG DESCRIPTION
BRICK BLITZ is a free breakout-style arcade game set inside a roaring pottery kiln. Slide your paddle to bounce a red-hot clay bead into a wall of glaze bricks and shatter every one of them before you run out of lives.

Every brick has its own glaze: normal bricks break in one hit, hard stoneware darkens before it shatters, gold-glazed bricks pay out big points, and blast bricks explode the neighbors around them. Chain three hits without dropping the bead and the whole kiln bursts into KILN FIRE — a full-screen warm glow that speeds up the bead for a hot scoring streak.

Catch falling glazes to widen your paddle, split into extra beads, or punch straight through bricks. Twelve handcrafted levels across three kilns — the Celadon, the Jun-Red and the Sky-Blue — add moving brick columns, a descending wall that ends the run if it reaches the floor, and a final blaze that stacks every rule at once.

Three stars per level: clear the kiln, keep your lives, and chase a perfect firing. Easy to learn in ten seconds, tricky to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.

每关三星：清窑、保命、打出完美开窑。十秒上手，越玩越上瘾。鼠标、触屏、键盘全支持，桌面与手机均可游玩。无需下载，浏览器免费畅玩。
