# CORGI DASH — Sheep-Herding Runner

A corgi charges across the Welsh countryside on an endless run. The world scrolls by itself — your only job is timing. Tap or press Space to jump fences, hay bales and puddles; hold the jump to leap higher, or press again mid-air for a double jump. Duck under low bridges by simply staying on the ground. Collect sheep as you run — they trail behind you in a living chain, and every sheep multiplies your score. The longer your chain, the bigger your reward, and the more you lose when you crash.

Crash into an obstacle and you shed half your flock instead of dying. Run out of sheep and the next hit ends your run. Land a jump within 10 pixels of an obstacle's top edge and you score a *perfect landing* — chain two in a row to unleash the **Sheep Surge**, a flock-wave rush that floods the screen with bonus points. When a row of obstacles threatens your whole chain, double-tap or press S to whistle: your flock scatters and slips past unharmed, but the whistle takes 8 seconds to recharge, so spend it on the moment that matters.

Three scene packs shift the mood as you advance — misty green meadows, golden wheat waves, and blue-grey storm hills — each with its own palette, weather and decorations. Twelve legs push the obstacle density higher and introduce new hazards: fences, hay bales, puddles, low bridges, double fences, moving sheep pens and full-width rows.

**Controls**

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jump (hold for higher, tap again to double-jump) | Space / W / ↑ | Tap |
| Whistle (scatter flock, 8s cooldown) | S / ↓ | Double-tap |
| Pause / resume | Esc / P | — |
| Restart (after game over) | R | Button |

柯基在威尔士草原上自动横跑，你只需要掌握节奏：点击或按空格跳过栅栏、滚草和水坑，长按跳得更高，空中再按一次完成二段跳；桥洞则要贴地通过、不要起跳。跑过羊群把它们收进身后的长链——羊越多分数倍率越高，但撞障碍时损失也越大。撞上障碍会先损失一半羊群而不是立刻判负，羊掉光后再撞一次才结束本局。

落点与障碍顶部相差 10 像素以内算一次「完美落点」，连续两次完美落点触发**牧羊冲鸭**：整条羊链掀起浪涌、屏幕爆出奖励分。面对横排障碍时双击或按 S 吹口哨，让羊群散开躲避——口哨有 8 秒冷却，要留给最关键的瞬间。晨雾草原（青绿）、麦浪（金）、暴雨山丘（蓝灰）三套场景随进度切换，十二段推进不断加入新障碍并加密。
