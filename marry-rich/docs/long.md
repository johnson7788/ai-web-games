**MARRY RICH** is a free browser game about one small-town girl and twelve life-changing decisions. You are the protagonist on her rise from intern to partner — every choice is a trade between three forces that pull against each other: **Ability**, **Network**, and **Savings**.

Each of the twelve chapters drops you into a moment of her life, from graduation to the boardroom. You pick between two or three options, watch your three attributes shift, and live with the consequence. Some choices are **timed** — a five-second countdown runs down while the golden **Life Scale** tilts toward the riskier path. Miss the deadline and the game quietly picks the safe, conservative option for you.

### How it plays
- **Choose** — click (or tap) an option. Every choice moves Ability, Network and Savings in different directions, and quietly builds hidden bonds with your mentor, your partner and your reputation.
- **Beat the clock** — key crossroads give you only five seconds. The Life Scale leans as you hesitate.
- **Find your destiny** — the final chapter computes one of **8 endings** from your attributes and bonds: from *Comeback Queen* to *A Quiet Return*.
- **What if…** — every ending unlocks a *memory shard* that lets you replay the road not taken, and your ending gallery fills up 1/8, 2/8, 3/8… as you explore every branch.

### Controls
- Mouse / touch: click an option, then click **Continue**.
- Keyboard: **1 / 2 / 3** to choose, **Space** or **Enter** to continue, **Esc** to pause, **R** to play again.

Free to play, no download, works on desktop and mobile. English and Chinese.
