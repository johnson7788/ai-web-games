# SLOT LEGENDS — Hero Reels

Spin the 5×3 reels and match six original hero symbols — Official, Escort, Wine Sage, Thief, Blade and Ingot — across up to 7 paylines to win chips. It's a pure-entertainment slot with **no real money**: your chips are "侠盗的传说筹码" (legend's chips) for fun only.

## What makes it different

- **聚义厅 Free Spins**: land 3+ Gathering Tokens (scatter) anywhere to trigger up to 20 free spins with a +50% multiplier.
- **押镖 Escort Bet**: before a spin, spend +50% extra chips to double your free-spin trigger rate, at the cost of −20% base payouts. A real risk/reward choice.
- **好汉令 Pity Jackpot**: collect 5 Gathering Tokens over time to guarantee a big winning line — a steady pity rhythm so you're never far from a highlight.
- **12-level progression**: unlock new icons, more paylines, a growing paytable and cheaper escort bets as you spin.
- **3 scene packs**: the warm-wood Tavern, the green Stronghold and the red-lantern Night Market — each chapter changes the whole palette.

## How to play

- Click / tap **SPIN** (or press **Space**) to roll; click **STOP** to halt the reels early.
- Pick your bet with the chip buttons (1–100).
- Toggle **Escort** (unlocks at level 3) to gamble the free-spin trigger.
- Land 3+ matching symbols left-to-right on any payline to win.
