# CANNON ECHO — Echo Cannon Valley Defense

CANNON ECHO is a fast arcade defense game. You command an echo cannon at the bottom of a canyon fortress. Enemy crates pour down the valley in 12 waves — slow floating crates, fast dive clusters, supply crates hidden behind cover, and armored crates that shrug off direct hits.

Your cannon fires real projectiles with travel time. Shots bounce off the canyon walls and sweep horizontally as an **echo** — use that bounce to hit shielded and armored crates from the side, and chain one shot into three kills for a roaring valley triple-hit. But keep firing too long and the barrel **overheats**, locking you out for 1.5 seconds, so manage your heat rhythm.

Clear a wave and pick one of three upgrades: ricochet, pierce, echo chain, frost tips, boss buster, power, rate, coolant, and velocity. Three canyon chapters — the gold-green Morning Valley, the blue-white Snow Pass, and the red-black Lava Gorge — change the palette and the pressure.

**How to play:** Move the mouse (or drag your finger) to aim. Hold the mouse button (or hold your touch) to fire. To hit a shielded or armored crate, aim at the canyon wall at the same height and let the shot echo back. Stop firing before the heat bar fills. Don't let crates reach your fortress.

操作：移动鼠标 / 拖动手指瞄准，按住鼠标 / 按住触屏持续开火；打护盾或装甲目标时，瞄准与目标同高的崖壁让炮弹反弹横扫；热量快满时停手冷却。
