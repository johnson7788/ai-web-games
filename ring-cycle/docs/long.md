# RING CYCLE · Candy Factory Faller

RING CYCLE is a fast, colorful arcade faller. You steer a candy ball down a spinning array of colored rings. Your color is your lifeline: land on the ring segment matching your color to score and keep your streak, chain five same-color rings to unleash a **rainbow candy rain**, and use white rings as a universal safe shortcut or wooden rings as a one-time bounce to escape a tight spot. The rings get meaner across 12 bands — sparse, moving, color-swapping, narrow, and white-scarce — as you fall through three candy worlds: the Fruity Workshop, the Mint Vault, and the Glow Vault.

## Controls

- **Steer**: Left / Right arrow keys (or A / D), or drag / touch the screen.
- **Pause**: Esc or P. **Restart**: R (after spilling).
- Touch is fully supported; play in landscape for the best view.

RING CYCLE（彩环蹦蹦）是一款快节奏街机下落游戏。你操控一颗糖球穿过旋转的彩环阵：颜色就是生命线——落在与你颜色相同的环上得分并保持同色链，连吃 5 个同色环触发「彩虹糖雨」；白环是万能安全捷径，木环是一次性跳板，能在关键时刻救急。12 个推进带会轮换稀疏环、移动环、变色环、窄格环与无白环，一路穿过果味车间、薄荷库、夜光仓三大场景。

## 操作

- 移动：← → 方向键（或 A / D），触屏直接拖动。
- 暂停：Esc / P。死亡后按 R 重开。
- 建议横屏游玩。
