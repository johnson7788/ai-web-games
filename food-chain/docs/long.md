# FOOD CHAIN — Glowing Deep-Sea Food Web

FOOD CHAIN is a deep-sea arcade game of size and survival. You start as a tiny plankton drifting through a glowing ocean. Swim toward glowing plankton clouds and smaller fish to eat them — every bite grows your mass, and every mass threshold evolves you up the food chain: plankton → krill → glow fry → reef darter → lantern fish → viper wrasse → abyss hunter → ghost ray → fang eel → vent lurker → leviathan → apex light. Touch a fish bigger than you and you are the one being eaten.

Three scene packs change the whole world as you climb: the bright-cyan Photic Zone with sun rays and coral silhouettes, the deep-blue Twilight Zone with drifting silt and bioluminescent specks, and the dark-red Vent Zone with hydrothermal plumes and a rushing dark current.

Two decisions keep every run tense. The toxic stingfish looks smaller than you — you *can* eat it, but doing so shrinks you, so "edible" is not always "should eat". And a jellyfish offers symbiosis: hug it for 3 seconds to gain a protective sting that lets you eat one stage bigger. Eating a same-level rival triggers your signature light-chain evolution flash.

**Controls:** move with the mouse / touch (drag) or WASD / arrow keys. Pause with Esc, restart with R. On mobile, drag to steer; rotate to landscape for the best view.

《FOOD CHAIN》是一款深海食物网题材的街机吞噬游戏。你从一只发光的浮游生物开始，游向浮游群和比自己小的鱼进食，体型随进食不断变大，跨过体型阈值就进化成更高的食物链阶段——浮游、磷虾、发光鱼苗、礁石飞鱼、灯笼鱼……一路直到顶点之光。碰到比你大的鱼就会立刻被吃掉，一局结束。

随着进化，整个海底世界会切换三套场景：亮青色的光合带（阳光光束与珊瑚剪影）、深蓝色的中层带（漂沙与生物荧光点）、暗红色的热泉带（热泉羽流与暗流区）。两条核心决策让每局都紧张：**毒刺鱼**看起来比你小、能吃，但吃下会掉体型——「能吃」不等于「该吃」；**水母共生**——贴近水母 3 秒获得护体毒刺，期间可以吃掉比自己大一级的鱼。吞下同级对手会触发「光链进化闪光」，这是本作的招牌瞬间。

操作：鼠标 / 触屏拖动或 WASD / 方向键移动；Esc 暂停；R 重开。移动端拖动转向，建议横屏获得最佳体验。
