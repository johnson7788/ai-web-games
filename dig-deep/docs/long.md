# DIG DEEP — Drill to the Core

DIG DEEP is an original arcade digger that turns "falling through numbered blocks" into an **active vertical drill**. You pilot a rig above a five-lane shaft and dig your own path down through the Earth, one cell at a time.

**How to play**
- **Move** ◀ ▶ (or A/D, or drag) to pick a column.
- **Dig** Space / S / ↓ (or tap) to break the cell below.
- **Switch tools** with 1 / 2 / 3: Shovel (power 1, cost 3), Drill (power 2, cost 2), Laser (power 5, cost 1 — cuts rock, limited charges).

**The loop**
- Cells have hardness: tap repeatedly, or use a stronger tool to one-shot them.
- **Rock** is unmineable except by Laser. **Lava** restarts the layer if you touch it.
- **Diamonds** restore energy + score; **fossils** restore more energy and give a Laser charge.
- A **rising flood** chases you from the surface — if it catches you, you drown. Keep digging down.
- **Fault lines** collapse under a shovel (you take damage and the cells below turn to rubble) — plan your tool choice.

**Progression**: 12 layers across three original scene packs — warm-brown Loam (1–4), cyan-black Fossil River (5–8), and red-gold Molten Core (9–12). Each layer adds a twist: hard-rock arrays, a faster dark river, giant fossil puzzle cells, diamond lodes, lava pockets, rising magma. Dig all the way to the core for the signature moment — molten gold floods the shaft and you unlock the **"You Dug Through the Earth"** achievement.

《挖穿地心》是一款原创街机挖掘游戏：把"数字砖块下落"改成**主动向下钻探**。你操控一台钻机，在 5 列竖井里点击/滑动选格下挖，一路挖穿地球。

- **移动**：方向键 ← →（或 A/D、拖动）选列。
- **挖掘**：空格 / S / ↓（或点按）打破下方格子。
- **换工具**：1 / 2 / 3 —— 铲子（威力 1、耗体力 3）、钻头（威力 2、耗体力 2）、激光（威力 5、耗体力 1，可切岩，需化石充能）。

格子有硬度，多次点击或换更强工具可秒挖；障碍岩只有激光能切；熔岩碰到就重开本层。钻石化石回体力、记分，化石还给激光充能。洪水从地表上涨追赶你，追到就淹死，必须不停向下挖。断层用铲子挖会塌方砸伤并把下方变成瓦砾——规划好工具选择。12 层深度、三大原创场景包（黄土层/化石暗河/熔核），挖到地心触发熔金溢流与「你挖穿了地球」成就。
