CATEGORY

TAGS

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Weave a glowing spider-silk path across star webs — visit every star once, in one unbroken thread.

LONG DESCRIPTION
INK PATH is a free one-stroke puzzle game where a firefly spider weaves silk across starry constellations in a night sky.

Start at the glowing star, then weave from star to star along the silk threads without lifting a finger. Every star must be visited exactly once, and every move commits your thread — take a wrong fork and you must backtrack and reweave. Light the whole web to win, and the entire constellation ripples with light while a swarm of fireflies bursts free.

12 handcrafted webs across three worlds: Dusk Forest, Aurora Ice and Deep Space. Master star wheels, bridge graphs, twin rings and dense constellations, then face the advanced weaves — one-way threads that only flow with the arrow, broken lines that unlock only after you light the trigger star, and edge-only webs where every thread must be woven exactly once.

Earn up to three stars per level by weaving in the fewest moves. Easy to learn in ten seconds, tricky to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download — play free in your browser.
