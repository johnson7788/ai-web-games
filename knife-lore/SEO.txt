KNIFE LORE is a free ink-wash blade game about a scroll-dojo master who cleaves spinning scroll posts with thrown shuriken. Clear all your blades without nicking one already stuck and the post splits in a slow-motion bloom of blade-light and ink. 12 levels across the Bamboo Garden, Dusk City and Snow Peak, with square posts, twin posts, moving posts, speed pulses and direction reversals. Every three levels earn Iron Cut to reveal a guaranteed safe angle. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard. No download, play free.
