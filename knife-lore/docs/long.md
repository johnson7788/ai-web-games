KNIFE LORE is a free ink-wash blade game about a scroll-dojo master who cleaves spinning scroll posts with thrown shuriken.

A scroll post spins at the center of the screen. Tap (or press Space) to throw one shuriken at a time. Each blade sticks at the exact angle it lands and starts carving an ink ring into the post. Throw all your blades without nicking one that is already stuck, and the post splits in two in a slow-motion bloom of blade-light and ink. Hit your own blade and it shatters — one mistake ends the level.

12 handcrafted levels across three worlds: the green Bamboo Garden, the vermilion Dusk City and the blue-white Snow Peak. Round posts teach the core throw, square posts tighten the timing, twin posts dare you to land symmetric pairs for bonus points, and moving posts force you to lead the drift. Speed pulses, direction reversals and acceleration windows keep the rhythm shifting.

Every three levels you earn Iron Cut: spend it to reveal a guaranteed safe angle for one throw — save it for a fast post or the final blade.

Ten seconds to learn, hard to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.

《KNIFE LORE》是一款免费的水墨飞刀游戏：你是卷轴道场的刀客，向旋转的卷轴桩掷出手里剑。

屏幕中央的卷轴桩不停旋转。点击（或按空格）一次掷出一把手里剑，刀会插在你出手瞬间的角度上，并在桩上刻下一圈墨痕。不撞到任何已插下的刀、掷完全部飞刀，卷轴桩就会在慢动作刀光圆环中绽裂成两半、墨汁四溅；撞到自己插下的刀则刀崩判负，本关结束。

12 个手工关卡横跨三大场景：竹庭（绿）、暮城（朱红）、雪岳（蓝白）。圆桩教你核心循环，方桩收紧时机，双桩诱惑你追求对称加成，移动桩逼你预判漂移。加速脉冲、间歇反转让节奏不断变化。

每 3 关解锁一次「斩铁」：揭示一个必中的安全角度，用在快桩还是终局，由你决定。

十秒上手，越玩越烧脑。鼠标、触屏、键盘全支持，桌面与手机均可游玩。无需下载，浏览器免费畅玩。
