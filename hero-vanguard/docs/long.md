# HERO VANGUARD — Twin-Hero Station Defense

Command two heroes — **Blade** (melee) and **Bow** (ranged) — and hold the last orbital station against 12 waves of alien boarders. Enemies push in from the right and the top; if they reach your station they tear it apart, and when the base breaks, the line is lost.

Your heroes auto-attack anything in range, but the real power is in **you**: move the controlled hero into the breach, press **Tab/Q** to swap — the departing hero dashes to the new position and cuts everything in its path (resonance swap), press **J** to unleash a **Resonance Slash** (wider when both heroes stand together), and press **K** to **Shield Charge** — stand still for 2 seconds to channel a station shield while your hero stops attacking. Every kill drops gold, and between waves you pick one of three upgrades, including rule-changing ones like resonance radius +50%, swap damage ×2, or bow pierce.

Three scene packs change the whole look as you advance: **Near Orbit** (blue-white), **Asteroid Belt** (grey-orange), and **Deep Space** (purple-black).

**Controls:** WASD / arrow keys move · J resonance slash · K shield charge · Tab/Q swap hero · Esc pause · R redeploy. Touch: left joystick + on-screen skill buttons.

指挥双子英雄——近战「刃」与远程「弓」——守住最后的轨道站，抵御 12 波异星来敌。敌人从右路与上空推进，一旦摸到基地就开始拆防线，基地破即败。英雄会自动攻击射程内的敌人，但真正的力量在你手里：移动主控英雄堵口，按 Tab/Q 换位（离场英雄冲向新位置，沿途斩敌——换位本身就是攻击），按 J 释放共鸣斩（双英站位靠近时范围更大），按 K 站桩充能 2 秒给基地套护盾（充能期间自身不输出）。击杀得金币，波间三选一升级，含「共鸣范围 +50%」「换位伤害翻倍」「弓再穿透」等规则型成长。三场景包随章节更换：近轨（蓝白）/ 小行星（灰橙）/ 深空（紫黑）。

操作：WASD / 方向键移动 · J 共鸣斩 · K 护盾充能 · Tab/Q 换人 · Esc 暂停 · R 重新部署；触屏为左摇杆 + 屏幕技能按钮。
