**HOLE MUNCH** is a free browser arcade game where you are a black hole tearing through a midnight city. Swallow everything smaller than you — street lamps, sedans, buses, buildings, even whole skyscrapers — and grow bigger with every bite. You are not alone: five rival holes are eating the same city, and the biggest mouth wins.

### How it plays
- **Move** — drag with mouse or finger (or WASD / arrow keys). Your hole glides toward the pointer.
- **Eat to grow** — anything smaller than your hole gets sucked in and adds to your mass. Bigger you, bigger prey.
- **Big holes eat small holes** — chase rival holes when you're larger, and flee when you're not. Get swallowed and the match ends.
- **Finish top 3** — every match is a 2-minute ranked race against 5 AI holes. Rank in the top 3 to advance.
- **Gravity chain** — swallow 10 things in the same area within 3 seconds and your gravity well expands 30% for 5 seconds.
- **Meteor rush** — every 30 seconds a meteor shower falls; meteor fragments grow you 3× faster.
- **Three districts** — Old Town (warm gold), the Harbor (cyan blue), and Future City (neon pink-purple), each with a new skyline and new prey.

### Why it's different
- **The signature moment**: swallowing a whole skyscraper — its debris spirals into your hole as the screen ripples with a shockwave.
- **Speed is a cost**: the bigger you get, the slower you move. Size is power, not comfort.
- **Every chase is a wager**: rush the high-value building blocks, or sweep the safe street corners while a bigger rival circles.

Free to play, no download. Works on desktop and mobile. English and Chinese.
