HOW TO PLAY

Tilt the floating track so your glass marble rolls to the glowing goal hole. Grab gems for points, beat the clock for stars — and don't fall into the void!

  • WASD or Arrow keys tilt the whole track. The marble rolls downhill.
  • Gems = 100 points each. Checkpoint rings save your respawn spot.
  • Chevron pads and ramps launch you over gaps. Keep your speed!
  • Amber platforms move — ride them. Orange sweepers will knock you off.
  • Falling costs one of 3 lives. Lose them all and the run ends.
  • Space = pause · R = retry level · M = mute. On touch: drag anywhere to tilt.
