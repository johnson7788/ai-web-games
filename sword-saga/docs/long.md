# SWORD SAGA — Song of the Cloud-Hidden Sword

**SWORD SAGA** is a turn-based wuxia RPG told as a 12-chapter short story. You play Yun Jian, a wandering swordsman, together with Linghu, a spirit fox. Walk the tile map, speak with wayfarers, and duel monsters in speed-bar turn-based battles. Every enemy carries one of the five elements — Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth — and the five-element wheel decides who wins: a countering element strikes for 1.5× damage and paints the whole screen in a wash of colored ink.

Two original mechanics define the combat:

- **Sword Intent (剑意蓄势)** — defending does not waste your turn. It charges your sword, doubling the damage of your next skill. Defend, then strike.
- **Fox Substitute (灵狐替身)** — when a killing blow is about to land on Yun Jian, Linghu can take the hit for him, then leaves the field for 2 turns. It is a limited save resource: spend it too early and you lose your best damage dealer.

## How to play

- **Desktop:** Arrow keys (or WASD) to walk. Click menu buttons to fight. Enter / Space to advance dialogue. Esc to pause.
- **Touch:** the on-screen D-pad moves you; battle and dialogue are tap-driven.

## 玩法简介（中文）

《云隐剑歌》是一款回合制武侠 RPG，用 12 章短篇讲述少年剑客云隐与灵狐并肩游历三境、封印塔魔的故事。青竹林、雪山道、锁妖塔三套水墨场景，五行相克（金木水火土）决定胜负——克制命中伤害 ×1.5 并全屏墨晕。战斗为速度条出手，菜单点击选择普攻 / 技能 / 道具 / 防御 / 换人。

两大原创机制：**剑意蓄势**——防御回合蓄剑意，下回合技能伤害 ×2；**灵狐替身**——灵狐替主角挡致命一击，之后 2 回合离场，是稀缺的保命资源。

## 操作

- 桌面：方向键 / WASD 行走，鼠标点击菜单战斗，Enter / 空格推进对话，Esc 暂停。
- 移动端：屏幕方向键行走，触屏点击战斗与对话。
