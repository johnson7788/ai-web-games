# COLOR FLUX

Tap to bounce an aurora firefly through spinning polar rings. Your firefly has one color — only the ring segment of the same color lets you pass. Hit the wrong color and the run is over.

## How to play

- **Tap / Space** — bounce the firefly upward.
- **Hold** — hover (slow your fall) to wait for the matching segment to rotate into place.
- **Color cores** — fly into a glowing orb to change your firefly's color.
- **Dual cores** — fly into a two-ring core to pass two colors for 5 seconds (score is halved while it lasts).
- **Rhythm rings** — every 5th ring pulses to a beat; pass it only on the beat.
- **Aurora burst** — chain 10 clean passes to unleash a full-screen aurora flood.

Score is endless. Each ring passed is +1. Your best score is saved on this device.

## 中文说明

点按让极光萤起跳，穿过旋转的极光环带。萤只有一种颜色——只有与它同色的环段才可通过，撞到异色即失败。

**操作**：点按 / 空格起跳；按住悬浮减速，等待正确颜色转到位；吃色核变色；吃双色核心可在 5 秒内通行两种颜色（期间得分减半）；每 5 环出现一个韵律环，必须踩着节拍穿过；连续穿 10 环触发全屏「极光爆发」。

无尽计分，每穿一环 +1 分，最高分保存在本设备。
