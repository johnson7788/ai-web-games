# CONSTELLATION LINE

You are a stargazer in an observatory dome. Scattered stars wait to become a constellation — but you may only draw each line **once**. Starting from the pulsing star, drag or tap from star to star to trace a single continuous path that covers every glowing line. Finish the last stroke and the whole constellation ignites and lifts off across the sky.

## How to play

- **Drag / tap** a star to draw the next line from your current star.
- Each **line can be drawn only once**. A wrong move resets your current segment.
- **Meteors** sweep across late levels and block a line for a short window — route around or wait.
- **Twin-star maps** split into two figures: finish one, then switch your brush to the other.
- Beat the **timer** and keep **misses** low for up to three stars per constellation.

## Features

- 24 constellation levels across 12 star charts (5 → 18 stars).
- 4 edge patterns: open path, closed loop, north-star waypoint, twin subgraphs.
- 3 hand-built scene packs: Spring Night (blue-violet), Summer Milky Way (bright white), Aurora Winter (teal-green).
- Procedural WebAudio soundtrack and sound effects — no downloads.

## 星座连线（中文说明）

你是天文台的描星员：散落的星星等着被连成星座，但**每条线只能画一次**。从发光的起点星出发，拖动或点按相邻的星，一笔连完所有线段。最后一笔落下，整座星座会亮起并飞过夜空。

- **拖动 / 点按**星星，从当前星连到下一条线。
- 每条**线段只能走一次**，走错会重置当前段。
- 高关卡会有**流星**划过、遮住某段线约 2 秒——绕行或等窗口。
- **双星图**分成两个独立子图，画完一半后切换画笔到另一半。
- 限时完成、减少失误，可获得最多 3 星评价。

共 24 关、12 座星座（5→18 颗星），四种边型轮换，三个原创场景包（春夜蓝紫 / 夏银河亮白 / 极光冬青绿），全部音乐与音效为程序化合成。
