# GOBLIN MINES

You are the foreman of a night-shift goblin mining town. Swap adjacent ore veins to make matches of three, mine ruby, sapphire, amethyst, emerald, fuel and gold, and clear the rock walls that block your way. When the shaft is clear, press **Dig deeper** to drop six metres, unlock the next vein, and push toward the twelfth and final mine beast.

Hire lantern goblins with your gold and light the columns you want to mine faster — a lit column yields bonus ore and slowly auto-mines for you. Watch your fuel: every lantern drains it, and digging deeper costs fuel too. When a cave-in warning flashes, spend 8 gold to **Reinforce** the shaft, or **Gamble** and risk losing stability. Run out of moves or stability, and the mine collapses on you.

## Controls

- **Drag / swipe** an ore onto a neighbour to swap it.
- **Tap a skill** to trigger it (TNT and Precise Pick ask you to tap a tile).
- **Dig deeper** when the rock walls are cleared.
- **Hire goblin** lights a column; tap a goblin to upgrade it.
- **Esc / P** pause · **R** restart · **Enter** start.

你是深夜矿镇的地精工头：交换相邻矿脉凑成三连，开采红宝石、蓝宝石、紫晶、绿宝石、燃料与金块，炸开挡路的岩壁。清空岩壁后按下「向下挖」下潜六米，解锁新矿脉，一路挖向第十二层、击杀最终矿兽。

用金币雇佣提灯地精，照亮你想加速开采的矿道——被照亮的竖列产出加成，还会缓慢自动挖矿。留意燃料：每盏灯都在消耗燃料，下挖也要燃料。塌方预警出现时，花 8 金币「加固」矿洞，或「赌一把」承受安全度损失。步数耗尽或安全度归零，矿洞就会塌方。

**操作**：拖动/滑动矿石与相邻格交换；点技能触发（TNT 与精准采集需要再点一格）；岩壁清空后「向下挖」；「雇佣地精」点亮一列，点地精升级；Esc/P 暂停，R 重开，Enter 开始。
