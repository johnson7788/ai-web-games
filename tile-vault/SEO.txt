CATEGORY

TAGS

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Stack treasure tiles in a 3D vault, match three of a kind, and rain coins into the bag before the tray overflows.

LONG DESCRIPTION
TILE VAULT is a free 3D tile-matching puzzle game about a cat burglar raiding a treasure vault.

Treasures are stacked in a pseudo-3D isometric pile — gem boxes, golden goblets, ancient coins, crowns and more. Only the pieces on top can be picked: tap an uncovered treasure, drop it into the bottom tray, and match three of the same kind to melt them into a shower of coins that fall into your money bag. Fill the tray past its limit or run out of time, and the heist is over.

12 handcrafted levels across three worlds: the Underground Vault, the Tomb Corridor and the Dragon Hoard. Layer stacks grow deeper, treasure kinds climb from 6 to 12, and the tray shrinks from 7 slots to 6. Deep well columns, twin stacks, fake locks, timed alarms and limited undo/shuffle tools keep every raid fresh.

Three stars per level: clear the vault, keep time to spare, and finish without burning a retry. Rotate the camera, plan your pick order, and master the tray.

Easy to learn in ten seconds, tricky to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download — play free in your browser.

十秒上手，越玩越烧脑。鼠标、触屏、键盘全支持，桌面与手机均可游玩。无需下载，浏览器免费畅玩。
