# NEO TETRA

NEO TETRA is a neon alchemy-furnace take on classic Tetris. Every block is an element crystal — copper, iron, gold, diamond — and every cleared line is a fresh pour from the furnace. Chain consecutive clears to build heat; fill the heat bar and the furnace erupts into a 10-second **white-hot** state where score doubles but the crystals fall faster. Clear four lines at once to trigger a full-screen **TETRA eruption** with golden ingot splatter.

## How to play

- **← / → or A / D** — move the crystal
- **↑ / X / W** — rotate clockwise · **Z** — rotate counter-clockwise
- **↓ / S** — soft drop · **Space** — hard drop
- **C** — hold / swap the current crystal
- **Esc / P** — pause

On touch devices, swipe left/right to move, swipe down to soft drop, tap to rotate, and use the on-screen HOLD / DROP buttons.

## The climb

Work through 12 mine layers, each with a target number of lines and a rotating twist: accelerated veins, double next preview, dark wells where only the ghost glows, narrow wells, heavy stone blocks that can't be cleared, and gift blocks that drop random bonuses. Three visual scene packs — warm copper, cold ice, and red-black melt core — each bring their own palette and music layer.

## 中文说明

NEO TETRA 是一款霓虹炼金炉主题的俄罗斯方块：方块是元素晶块，消行等于熔炼出炉。连续消行会蓄热，热力拉满进入 10 秒「白热态」——分数翻倍但下落加速；一次消四行触发「熔炉喷发」全屏流光与金锭飞溅。

**操作**：←/→ 移动，↑/X 旋转，Z 反转，↓ 软降，空格硬降，C 暂存。触屏用滑动手势：左右滑移动、下滑软降、点按旋转，另有 HOLD/DROP 按钮。

**推进**：12 个矿层，每层有目标行数与特性轮换（加速矿脉 / 双 next / 暗井 / 窄井 / 重块 / gift 块），三大场景包（铜矿暖橙 / 冰晶青蓝 / 熔核红黑）各有专属视觉与 BGM 层。
