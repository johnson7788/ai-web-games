CATEGORY

TAGS

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Tap the falling neon pulses on the beat across four energy lanes and chain 8 PERFECTs to enter Groove Mode.

LONG DESCRIPTION
PULSE TAP is a free neon rhythm game set on a heartbeat nightclub DJ booth.

Four glowing energy lanes drop colored pulses toward a judgment line. Press D, F, J, K (or tap the lanes on touch) exactly on the beat to score PERFECT, build combos, and keep your health bar alive. Missed pulses drain your health — clear the whole track before it empties to win the set.

Chain 8 PERFECTs in a row to trigger GROOVE MODE: the whole booth breathes with the beat and the judgment window widens. Master tap notes, hold notes that glow while you press, slide chains that jump between lanes, and gray fake notes you must not touch.

12 handcrafted tracks across three neon worlds — Neon Bar, Rooftop Electro and Midnight Subway. Each stage adds a new twist: tempo shifts, density climbs, decoy notes, two-hand chords, syncopation and a full-song finale.

Three stars per stage: clear the track, stay accurate, and keep the combo unbroken. Easy to learn in ten seconds, hard to perfect. Works with keyboard, mouse and touch. No download — play free in your browser.
