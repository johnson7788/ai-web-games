# FOG TOWN — Oil-Lamp Detective

FOG TOWN is a fog-of-war grid puzzle. You are an oil-lamp detective crossing a misty town of 12 blocks. Each step burns oil to light a small radius around you, peeling back the fog tile by tile. Collect the key, flip the lever, and assemble three clues to unlock the exit door — then reach it with as few steps as possible.

Every step is a decision: do you cut straight through the fog to save oil, or detour along lit ground to avoid traps? Stand still for one turn and press **Echo** to hear whether a trap or lever hides within two tiles. In later blocks, guards patrol vision cones (step in and you are caught), and moving fog re-hides old ground, forcing you to navigate from memory. Assemble three clues to trigger the **Truth Moment** — the whole map lights up like a lighthouse for a few seconds.

**Controls:** Arrow keys / WASD or tap an adjacent tile to move · Space to echo · Esc to pause · R to retry. Three visual scenes rotate as you advance: Dusk Alley, Market Ruins, and Lakeside.

《雾町寻踪》是一款网格迷雾解谜游戏。你扮演提灯侦探，在 12 个街区中穿行：每走一步都会烧掉一点灯油，只照亮脚下的一小圈，逐步揭开迷雾。收集钥匙、拨动机关、拼齐三条线索来打开出口，并用最少的步数抵达出口。

每一步都是一次取舍：直穿迷雾省油，还是绕亮处避开陷阱？原地静止一步按「回声」，能听清相邻两格内有没有机关。后面的街区会出现守卫视线与「移动雾区」（旧格子重新起雾），逼你凭记忆规划路线。拼齐三条线索会触发「真相时刻」——整张地图像灯塔一样短暂全亮。三套场景随进度切换：暮巷、集市废墟、湖畔。

**操作：** 方向键 / WASD 或点按相邻格子移动 · 空格键回声 · Esc 暂停 · R 重开。
