Walk away and the forge keeps working — come back to offline earnings capped at 8 hours. Three hand-drawn forge worlds: a cozy village smithy, a stone castle workshop and a roaring volcano foundry.

Easy to learn in ten seconds, endlessly satisfying to master. Mouse, touch and keyboard supported on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.
