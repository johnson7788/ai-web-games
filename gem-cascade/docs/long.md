# GEM CASCADE

GEM CASCADE is a PopStar-style mineral vein puzzle. Tap a cluster of 2 or more same-colored gems to blast it and score `size² × 5`. After every blast the gems above fall down, empty columns slide left, and the vein keeps shifting. Reach the level's target score before the board locks up — clear every gem for a big bonus, or leave too many behind and the cave collapses on you.

Three mines rotate as you climb 12 levels: the purple **Fluorspar Cave**, the orange **Copper Pit**, and the cyan **Aurora Ice Cave** — each with its own palette, framing, and decorations. Every level adds one new rule: 6 gem colors, unbreakable edge rock, a double-scoring vein, sinking gems, limited moves, frozen gems that thaw only when you pop their neighbors, and rock pillars.

Two original twists give you a plan. **The Pickaxe** charges one tick for every blast; at full charge (5) it can smash any single gem to unblock a shape. **Wash** reshuffles the whole vein once per level, but it resets your streak — spend it to escape a dead board or save it for the perfect moment. Blast a group of 6 or more and the whole vein erupts in a **mineral cave-in** cascade of flowing light.

- **Click / tap** a gem cluster to blast it.
- **Pickaxe** button (charge 5) then tap any gem to smash it.
- **Wash** button to reshuffle the vein (1 per level, resets streak).
- **Esc / P** pause · **R** play again.

GEM CASCADE 是一款「消灭星星」式矿脉消除游戏。点击 2 个以上同色连通的宝石矿簇即可爆破，得分 = 数量² × 5；爆破后上方宝石下落、空列左移。在棋盘锁死之前达到关卡目标分——清空全部宝石有大额奖励，剩太多则矿脉塌陷。

12 关推进，三个场景包轮换：紫色**萤石洞**、橙色**铜矿坑**、青色**极光冰洞**，各有独立色板、构图与装饰。每关引入一个新规则：6 色矿脉、不可消的边界岩块、双倍矿脉、沉底宝石、限定步数、冰封宝石（需相邻消除解冻）、岩柱等。

两大原创机制：**矿镐**每爆破一次蓄力一格，满 5 格可劈碎任意单块疏通形状；**淘洗**每关一次重排矿脉，但会清零连消进度。6 连消触发**矿脉塌方**连锁流光，是全游戏最标志性的瞬间。

操作：点击/触摸矿簇爆破；点「矿镐」满蓄力后点单块劈碎；点「淘洗」重排；Esc 暂停；R 重开。
