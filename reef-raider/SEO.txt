Reef Raider — Free Deep-Sea Fishing Arcade Game

CATEGORY

TAGS

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Aim the reef cannon, fire glowing light-bulbs, and net fish to hit the target score across 12 underwater levels.

LONG DESCRIPTION
REEF RAIDER is a free deep-sea fishing arcade game where every shot is a firework.

You command a coral reef cannon. Move your mouse or finger to aim, click to fire a glowing light-bulb — each shot costs coins equal to your power level — and watch it burst into a colored light-net on impact. Catch lantern fish, butterfly fish, turtles, manta rays and the rare ghost lantern fish to earn coins and chase the level's target score before the clock runs out.

12 handcrafted levels across three underwater worlds: Coral Dawn, Reef Dusk and Phosphor Night. Fish speed up, dense swarms swarm the reef, rocks block your shots, coin-tight stages halve your wallet, combo streaks multiply your catch, and the final stage pits you against the Lantern Fish King. Switch cannon power from 1 to 5 anytime: big shots gamble on big fish, cheap shots mop up small swarms.

When a big fish is netted, it dissolves into a light-shadow that swims a lap around the screen — the signature deep-sea firework. Run out of coins and a relief refill keeps you fishing.

Easy to learn in ten seconds, hard to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.
