LONG DESCRIPTION
BALLOON BURST is a free lantern-popping puzzle game set at a sky-lantern festival.

Tap any group of two or more same-colored paper lanterns to burst them and send their light rising. The bigger the group, the higher the score. Lanterns float up to fill the gaps, and new same-color clusters trigger glowing chain bonuses — set up the board so one tap lights up a whole string.

12 handcrafted levels across three worlds: Dusk Gathering, Night Festival and Misty Valley. Metal lanterns need two hits to crack, spark lanterns ignite a whole row, chained lanterns must be freed together, shifting lanterns change color every few seconds, and stone weights sink to the bottom to block your moves. Big clears turn the burst lanterns into floating sky lanterns with a warm full-screen glow.

Reach the target score before the timer runs out. Three stars per level: hit the target fast and land a big clear for a perfect run. Ten seconds to learn, hard to master. Works with mouse and touch on desktop and mobile — no download, play free in your browser.
