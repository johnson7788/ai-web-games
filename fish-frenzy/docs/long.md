# FISH FRENZY

**FISH FRENZY** is a deep-sea bioluminescent fishing arcade. You command a glowing cannon at the bottom of the screen. Rotate to aim, click (or tap) to fire a net, and capture glowing deep-sea species — lanternfish, manta rays, hammerheads, and the rare oarfish. Your coin balance is your health: every shot costs your cannon tier, and running dry ends the run.

Two twists make each dive a gamble. A **fish-migration forecast** at the top of the screen predicts the route a school will swim five seconds ahead, so you can pre-aim your net. A **shock net** charges over 60 seconds and clears an entire formation in one area blast. Big fish pay huge multipliers but are much harder to catch — use a high cannon tier to gamble on the megamouth boss.

Clear 12 stages across three scene packs (the bright cyan coral farm, the deep-blue trench, and the dark-red hot vents). Each stage has a revenue target and a time limit; hit the target to advance before the timer or your coins run out.

**How to play**

- **Mouse / touch:** move to aim, click or tap to fire a net.
- **Keyboard:** Arrow keys / WASD aim · Space fires · `1`–`7` set cannon tier · `[` `]` lower/raise tier · `E` fires the shock net · `Esc` pauses · `R` restarts after a dive.

《FISH FRENZY》是一款深海夜光捕鱼街机游戏。你操控屏幕底部的发光炮台，旋转瞄准、点击发射渔网，捕获灯笼鱼、魔鬼鱼、锤头鲨、皇带鱼等发光深海物种。金币余额就是血量——每发炮弹按炮档扣币，打空判负。顶部「鱼汛预报」会提前 5 秒预告下一群鱼的路线，让你提前站位；「电击网」每 60 秒充能一次，可群捕整队鱼。大鱼倍率高但捕获率低，用高档炮豪赌巨口鲨 Boss。12 场推进结构，三套场景包（亮青珊瑚渔场、深蓝深沟、暗红热泉），每场都有营收目标与限时。

操作：移动鼠标/手指瞄准，点击发射；方向键或 WASD 瞄准，空格发射，数字键 1–7 换炮档，`[` `]` 微调炮档，`E` 释放电击网，`Esc` 暂停，`R` 重开。
