TITLE
Note Rider — Free Rhythm Slash Game

CATEGORY
Action

TAGS
rhythm, music, slash, knight, horse, string demons, beat, timing, arcade, browser game

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Ride a note-horse across the staff, switch strings and slash string demons right on the beat.

LONG DESCRIPTION
NOTE RIDER is a free rhythm action game about a string knight galloping across a five-line music staff on a magical note-horse.

Notes race toward the golden line in time with the music. Switch between the two strings with the arrow keys or a swipe, and slash exactly when a note lands on the line — hit the beat to strike the oncoming string demons, and chain eight PERFECTs to unleash the Chord Slash that shakes the whole staff.

12 handcrafted levels across three worlds: the Dawn String Bridge, the Rainy Night Piano City and the Star Sea Scale. Learn single notes, held charge slashes, two-string switching, red counter notes you must dodge, and tempo shifts — then face the Silent Demon King in the finale. Choose from shield, burst and lifesteal upgrades at the skill points to build your own run.

Easy to learn in ten seconds, hard to master on the beat. Play with keyboard or touch on desktop and mobile. No download, free in your browser.

LONG DESCRIPTION
《琴弦骑士》是一款免费节奏动作游戏：弦之骑士骑着一匹音符马，在五线谱桥上疾驰。

音符踏着节拍冲向金色判定线。用方向键或滑动切换上下两条弦，在音符落线的瞬间挥剑——命中节拍就是斩击迎面扑来的弦魔；连续 8 个完美判定，就能触发让整行五线谱震动的「和弦斩」。

12 个手工关卡横跨三大场景：晨曦弦桥、雨夜琴城、星海音阶。从单音、长按蓄斩、上下换轨，到必须躲开的红色反击音与变速乐章，一路打向终章的「寂静魔王」。在技能三选一升级点，用护盾、爆发或吸血构筑属于你的打法。

十秒上手，越打越有节奏。鼠标、触屏、键盘全支持，桌面与手机均可游玩。无需下载，浏览器免费畅玩。
