SHORT DESCRIPTION

LONG DESCRIPTION
HOP CHARGE is a free one-tap arcade jump game about a little lantern-holding raccoon hopping across moonlit rooftops.

Press and hold to charge the lantern flame — the longer you hold, the farther you leap. Release to spring through the air in a clean arc and land on the next roof. Land dead-center and your combo doubles: 1, 2, 4, 8. Land off-center and the streak resets. Slip off the edge and the lantern drops.

12 handcrafted levels across three worlds: Dusk Tile Town, Rainy Lantern Street and Misty Mountain Peak. Fixed gaps teach you the rhythm, then the rules twist — platforms slide sideways, springs bounce you two roofs with a risky drift, ice makes your landing skid, broken roofs shrink the safe zone, and headwinds eat your distance.

Perfect landings burst the flame into a glowing halo; string a combo and the raccoon's tail catches fire. Three stars per level for clean, combo-heavy runs.

Easy to learn in ten seconds, hard to master. Mouse, touch and spacebar on desktop and mobile. No download — play free in your browser.
