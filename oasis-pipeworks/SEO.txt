CATEGORY

TAGS

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Grab falling pipe pieces and lay a water path across the desert before the valve bursts open.

LONG DESCRIPTION
OASIS PIPEWORKS is a free pipe-connection puzzle game about bringing water to a dying desert oasis.

The clock is ticking: grab pipe pieces from the sliding queue on the right, tap empty grid cells to lay them, and build an unbroken line from the intake to the oasis tap before the countdown ends and the water breaks loose. Watch your route fill with flowing water in real time — every elbow you placed pays off, every rock you ignored punishes you.

12 handcrafted levels across three worlds: Dawn Desert, Dusk Canyon and Night Oasis. Rocks force you to reroute, blooming flowers reward brave detours, cross pipes open dead corners, and the fox foreman cheers on every drop you deliver.

Three stars per level: clear the stage, water every flower, and finish with time to spare. One dig per run lets you fix a mistake — spend it wisely.

Easy to learn in ten seconds, tricky to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.
