HOW TO PLAY

Bowl 10 frames. Knock down all 10 pins for a STRIKE, or clear them in two rolls for a SPARE. Strikes and spares earn bonus points. Score as high as you can — a perfect game is 300!

  • Move the mouse or press ← → to position the ball across the lane.
  • Hold A or D to add hook (curve). The ball bends more as it nears the pins.
  • Hold Space or the left mouse button to charge power, then release to bowl. The meter oscillates — time it!
  • Strike (X) = 10 + next 2 rolls. Spare (/) = 10 + next roll. The 10th frame gives up to 3 rolls.
  • Press P or Esc to pause. M mutes sound.
