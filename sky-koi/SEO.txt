SHORT DESCRIPTION
Flap a koi through torii gates across a cloud sea, collect pearls, and skim the water for a free splash jump.

LONG DESCRIPTION
SKY KOI is a free one-button arcade game about a koi leaping through the gates of a cloud sea.

Tap or press Space to make the koi flap upward, and thread its body through the gap between vermilion torii pillars to score. Every gate is a decision: fly high and play it safe, or skim close to the water to charge a free splash jump — one wrong dip and the koi sinks.

Collect three pearls to earn an extra star, dodge narrow gates, moving gates, crosswind gusts and rising tidal waves. Twelve handcrafted levels span three painted worlds: Dawn Cloud Sea, Sakura Lake and Night Star River, each with its own palette and rules.

Three stars per level: clear the gates, collect every pearl, and brave the water for splash jumps. Easy to learn in ten seconds, tricky to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard. No download, play free in your browser.
