# STAR SLAYER

Pilot the **Iron Guard**, a lone mech defending a star-vessel deck, with your AI partner **Light Core** hovering at your side. Every shot you fire, the Light Core replays half a second later — lead your aim so the delayed copy lands. When you're cornered, press **L** to phase-swap positions with your partner. When your hull drops below 30%, press **K** to self-destruct the Core in an **Overload Protocol**: a full-screen blast that wipes every hostile, then leaves your partner offline for 20 seconds.

Fight through **12 waves** across three decks — the blue-white Bridge, the gray-orange Outer Hull, and the cyan-purple Reactor — with a **Core Behemoth** boss every third wave. Between waves, pick one of three skill cards to build an attack-flow or a synergy-flow loadout.

## How to play

- **WASD / Arrow keys** — move
- **J / Space** — hold to fire (auto-aims the nearest hostile)
- **L** — Phase Sync (swap places with the Light Core)
- **K** — Overload Protocol (needs hull < 30% and Core online)
- **Esc / P** — pause · **R** — restart

Touch: virtual joystick + FIRE / OVER / SYNC buttons.

驾驶机甲「铁卫」与 AI 伙伴「光核」并肩作战：你的每次射击，光核会在 0.5 秒后延迟复制一发——打提前量是核心技巧。被逼入死角时按 **L** 与光核相位同步换位；血量跌破 30% 时按 **K** 触发**超载协议**，自爆光核清屏，代价是光核离线 20 秒。

横跨舰桥（蓝白）、舰体外壳（灰橙）、反应堆（青紫）三大舰区，迎战 12 波敌群，每 3 波迎战核心巨兽。波间三选一技能卡，构筑攻击流或协同流。

## 操作

- **WASD / 方向键** 移动
- **J / 空格** 按住开火（自动瞄准最近敌人）
- **L** 相位同步（与光核换位）
- **K** 超载协议（血量 < 30% 且光核在线时可用）
- **Esc / P** 暂停 · **R** 重新开始

触屏：虚拟摇杆 + FIRE / OVER / SYNC 按钮。
