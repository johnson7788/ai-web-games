# SNAKE ARENA

SNAKE ARENA is a neon reimagining of the classic snake arena. You are a living data-stream serpent gliding through a glowing grid. Steer with the pointer or joystick, eat data nodes to grow longer, and climb the leaderboard in 90-second ranked matches against a pack of rival AI snakes.

**The core loop:** eat glowing data nodes to grow, boost to outrun danger, and cut off rival snakes — a head that touches another snake's body is eliminated, and the fallen serpent bursts into a rain of data you can absorb. Score is your length. Rank #1 before the timer runs out.

**Two signature twists:**

- **Overclock (boost)** burns your own length into glowing shards — leave them behind as bait or as a gift to your rivals. Risk made into resource.
- **EMP Pulse** (once your length passes 30) stuns nearby snakes for 1.5s with a 20s cooldown — a decisive offensive window.

**12-match campaign** across three visually distinct arenas — the blue Data Core, the orange-red Meltdown, and the purple-black Deep Net — with rising AI count, aggression, and food density. Between matches, pick one of three upgrades (steering speed / magnet radius / pulse cooldown, plus rule-changing perks).

**Controls:** mouse/pointer or WASD/arrows to steer, Space to boost, E to pulse; on touch, drag to steer and use the on-screen BOOST / PULSE buttons. Pause with Esc/P. English by default, full 中文 available.

# 中文简介

SNAKE ARENA 是经典贪吃蛇竞技场的霓虹再造：你是一条「数据流」光蛇，在发光网格里滑行。吃数据点变长、在 90 秒名次赛里与一群 AI 对手争夺长度榜第一。头撞到其他蛇的身体即被淘汰，而倒下的对手会碎成一团数据雨，被你的蛇头吸走——比分就是长度。

**两个签名机制**：超频（加速）会烧掉自己的长度、留下可被对手吃的光屑，把风险变成资源；长度超过 30 后可放一次电磁脉冲，震晕附近敌蛇 1.5 秒（冷却 20 秒）。

**12 场推进**横跨三套场景——数据核心（蓝）、熔断区（橙红）、深网（紫黑），AI 数量、攻击性与食物密度逐场轮换，场间三选一升级（转向速度 / 磁吸半径 / 脉冲冷却，外加改规则型强化）。

**操作**：鼠标/指针或 WASD/方向键转向，空格超频，E 脉冲；触屏拖动转向，点屏幕上的超频/脉冲按钮。Esc/P 暂停。默认英文，含完整中文。
