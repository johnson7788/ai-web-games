# WATER RITUAL

You are the owner of a small teahouse, and for twelve days every guest's cup is your ritual. Hold the teapot to pour: the water level climbs toward the golden line while the temperature slides down the gauge. Release exactly when the water reaches the line *and* the heat falls into the guest's window — green, black, or flower tea each wants its own temperature — and the cup blooms with a golden ripple. That is **一口回甘**, the perfect pour.

Every five orders the market floods with a rush hour. Chain perfect pours to trigger a **Full House** and double your tips. Choose your risks: serve a demanding noble guest for double coin, or stick to the patient regular. Spend the day's earnings on rule-changing upgrades — slow pour, ice reserve, a thermometer, a second chance — and keep your hearts from running out.

- **How to play:** hold (mouse / finger / Space) to pour, release to commit. Release too early and the cup is under-filled; hold too long and it overflows. Watch the water line and the temperature gauge together.
- **Mobile:** tap and hold the cup. Rotate to landscape for the best view.
- **Three scenes:** warm-white morning tea, green noon market, and indigo night talk.

你是茶馆老板娘，十二天里每一杯都是你的仪式。按住茶壶倒水：水位升向金色刻度线的同时，水温沿温度计一路下降。等水位贴线、水温正好落进客人的口味区间（绿茶 / 红茶 / 花茶各有各的水温）再松手——杯口荡开一圈金圈涟漪，就是「一口回甘」的完美一倒。

每五单一波客流高峰，连续完美触发「满堂彩」翻倍小费。你可以赌一把接挑剔的贵客换双倍金币，也可以稳接耐心的老茶客。用当日收入买改规则的升级（慢倒 / 冰镇 / 温度计 / 二次机会），别让三颗心耗尽。

- **操作：** 按住（鼠标 / 手指 / 空格）倒水，松手结算。松太早水位不够，按太久会溢出；水位线和温度计要一起看。
- **移动端：** 点住杯子即可倒水，横屏体验最佳。
- **三套场景：** 暖白晨茶、青翠午市、靛蓝夜谈。
