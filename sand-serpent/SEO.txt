SAND SERPENT is a free desert snake arcade game where you burrow through shifting dunes and eat the desert alive.

Steer a golden sand serpent across a 12-level desert journey. Every food has a trade-off: beetles are a safe +1 snack, scorpions are worth +3 but they scurry sideways when you chase them, and camel thorns give +5 while dropping one of your tail segments. Grow to the goal length to clear each stage — but watch out for border walls, cacti, hard ground, speed-boost tiles and the sandstorms that blind the starlit desert.

Three handcrafted worlds: Day Dunes, Oasis Edge and Starlit Desert. Twelve levels layer in new twists, from wrap-around edges to escape-proof mazes, so no two runs feel the same.

Signature touch: your serpent leaves quicksand ripples in its wake, and every bite bursts into a shower of sand. Easy to learn in ten seconds, tricky to master — play free in your browser with keyboard, WASD or touch swipe.
