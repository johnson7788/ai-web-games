# IDIOM FORGE

Fill the missing characters. Forge the blade.

IDIOM FORGE is a word-forging puzzle: each question shows a Chinese idiom (or a 4-letter English word) with its meaning, but 1–2 characters are missing. Tap the right tiles from the candidate grid to complete it. Every correct answer scores and builds your combo; every wrong answer costs time and drops the bad character into the slag — collect 3 slag to smelt a free hint.

Every 5 correct answers forge one of 12 legendary weapons (sword, bow, spear, axe, and more). Watch the furnace blast a weapon silhouette into the air when the characters click into place.

Three forge scenes change the whole look as you advance: the warm orange Blacksmith, the green bamboo Academy, and the gold-purple Sky Workshop. A riddle track appears about 30% of the time — a cryptic clue to guess, worth ×2 points.

## How to play

- Click a tile in the candidate grid to fill the current blank slot.
- Click the blinking slot's correct character to advance; wrong characters cost time.
- Use Hint to reveal the correct character (limited; or smelt 3 slag into one).
- Use Reset to swap the current question for a new one (limited; breaks your combo).
- Survive the timer across 12 levels; each level tightens the clock and grows the grid.
- Press Esc to pause, R to restart.

## 中文说明

成语熔炉（IDIOM FORGE）是一款「锻字成刃」的填字游戏：每题给出一个成语（或英文 4 字母单词）及其释义，但缺 1–2 个字。从候选字格点选正确字补全，答对连击得分，答错扣时并把错字丢进「炉渣」——集满 3 个炉渣可熔炼成一次提示。

每答对 5 题，就铸成一件神兵（剑、弓、枪、斧……共 12 件）；成语四字齐聚的瞬间，熔炉会喷出兵器剪影。三章场景随进度切换：暖橙铁匠铺、青竹书院、金紫天工阁；约 30% 概率出现「字谜模式」，看谜面猜单字，得分 ×2。

操作：点击候选字格填入当前空格；用「提示」亮出正确字（限量，或用 3 炉渣熔炼换取）；用「重置」换一题（限量，中断连击）；Esc 暂停，R 重开。
