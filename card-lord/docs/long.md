# CARD LORD — Cyber Landlord Card Game

CARD LORD is a single-player 1v2 landlord card game (斗地主) played on a neon cyber table. You face two AI opponents across a 12-tier ladder. Each round: **bid** 1 / 2 / 3 (or pass) to become the landlord, optionally **double** the stakes (up to ×64), then **play legal card combos** to beat the previous play — single, pair, triple, triple+1, straight, straight-pairs, plane, plane with wings, four+2, bomb, and rocket. A bomb lights the whole table red with a screen shake.

Two original twists make it more than a port:

1. **Card counter (记牌器)** — a live bar shows how many of each rank remain unplayed, so you can read the table instead of guessing. The AI reads it too.
2. **Doubling** — after bidding, one round of doubling lets you gamble the multiplier up to ×64.

Win a round to climb a tier; base score and AI strength rise every tier. Landlord wins without the farmers playing a single hand = **Spring** (×2). Farmers win while the landlord played only once = **Anti-Spring** (×2).

## How to play

- **Desktop**: click cards to select a combo, then **Play** / **Pass** / **Hint**. Bid with the 1 / 2 / 3 buttons. `Esc` pause, `R` retry, `Enter` start.
- **Touch**: tap cards and buttons — all controls are ≥44px.

## 中文说明

《牌王》是一款赛博霓虹牌桌风格的斗地主单机版：你 1v2 对战两名 AI，共 12 个段位。每局先**叫分**（1 / 2 / 3 或不叫）抢地主，可再**加倍**（倍数最高 ×64），然后**出合法牌型**压过上家——单、对、三、三带一、顺子、连对、飞机、四带二、炸弹、王炸。打出炸弹会全屏红光震屏。

两个原创改动：**记牌器**（实时显示每种点数剩余张数，AI 也会读）与**加倍**（心理博弈）。地主未让农民出过一手即**春天 ×2**；农民赢且地主只出过一手即**反春天 ×2**。胜利升段，底分与 AI 强度逐段提升。
