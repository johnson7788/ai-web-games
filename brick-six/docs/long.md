# BRICK SIX — Honeycomb Workshop

BRICK SIX is a relaxing-yet-spiky hexagonal block puzzle. Random honeycomb pieces (2–4 cells) appear in your queue. Tap a cell to place the glowing piece onto the hex board. Fill an entire ring — any of the 27 lines across the three directions — and it clears, scoring every cell inside it. Clear several rings at once and your combo multiplier soars; clear **three rings in a single drop** and you trigger the signature **Royal Jelly gold cascade**.

Two twists keep your spatial planning honest:

- **Wax-sealed cells** need to be cleared twice. A sealed cell shrugs off its first line clear — you have to rebuild the ring around it and clear again.
- **Pollen blocks** must be cleared within three moves of landing. Clear one in time and it infects and wipes its neighbours, chaining into a board-clearing bloom.

Push through **12 tiers** of rising score targets. Each chapter rotates the rules: the board narrows as wax walls close in, a second spawn port adds a fourth piece, wax density climbs, pollen floods the pool, and big 4-cell pieces crowd your queue. Three hand-built scene packs — the gold-green **Spring Nest**, the amber **Honey Vault**, and the blue-white **Winter Nest** — change palette and decoration as you climb.

## How to play

- **Desktop:** move the mouse to aim, click a cell to place. Press `R` (or the ↻ button) to rotate the piece, `Tab`/`E`/`Q` or `1–4` to switch pieces, arrow keys + `Space` to place with the keyboard, `Esc` to pause.
- **Touch:** tap a cell to place; tap the ↻ button to rotate.

## 中文说明

BRICK SIX（蜂巢工坊）是一款六边形堆叠消除游戏。随机蜂巢块组（2–4 格）会出现在候选队列里，点击棋盘上的格子即可放下发光的块组。铺满一整环（棋盘上 3 个方向共 27 条消除线）即消除得分；一次消多环会翻倍连消，**一次消满三环**即触发招牌「蜂王浆金瀑」。两大机制考验空间规划：**蜂窝封蜡**格需要消两次才能移除；**花粉块**落地后 3 步内被消除会传染清除相邻格子，形成连锁。12 个档位目标分递增、特性轮换（窄盘 / 双生成口 / 蜡密度升 / 花粉富集 / 大块占比），并随章节切换春巢（金绿）、蜜库（琥珀）、冬巢（蓝白）三套场景。

操作：桌面端移动鼠标瞄准、点击落位，按 `R` 或 ↻ 旋转、`Tab`/`E`/`Q`/`1–4` 换块、方向键 + `Space` 落位、`Esc` 暂停；触屏端点击格子落位、点 ↻ 旋转。
