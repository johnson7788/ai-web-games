SHORT DESCRIPTION
CELL WARS — a free petri-dish strategy duel. Infect adjacent cells, drop nutrient for chain spreads, and wipe enemy colonies with your phage across 12 dishes.

LONG DESCRIPTION
CELL WARS is a free petri-dish strategy game about a blue colony battling a red strain. Tap enemy or neutral cells next to your own to infect them — your power equals how many of your cells surround the target, and leftover power cascades outward in a glowing filament spread. Drop nutrient once per turn to buff next turn's infections, and fire a charged phage to wipe a 2×2 enemy area. Cells carry defense levels; failed infections still soften them. Clear every red cell or hold more territory at the round limit across 12 dishes in three scene packs: Classic (cyan-white), Resistance (gold-purple), and Mutation (red-black). Works with mouse and touch, English and Chinese, free in your browser.
