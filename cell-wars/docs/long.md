**CELL WARS** is a free petri-dish strategy game that runs in your browser. You command a colony of blue bacteria under a microscope and battle a rival red strain for control of the dish.

Your colony spreads by **infection**: tap any enemy or neutral cell next to your own blue cells, and your attack power equals how many of your cells surround it. Outpower the cell's defense and you capture it — and leftover power cascades outward in a glowing **filament spread**, lighting up neighbor colonies like neurons firing.

### How it plays
- **Infect** — tap an enemy or neutral cell beside your blue cells. Surround a cell to capture it in one blow and chain-capture its neighbors.
- **Nutrient** — once per turn, tap one of your own cells to drop a droplet. That cell and its four neighbors get +2 infect power next turn — invest now to set up a huge chain.
- **Phage** — charges every 3 turns. Tap the Phage button, then a cell, to wipe a 2×2 area of enemy colonies back to neutral.
- **Defense levels** — enemy cells and neutral fortresses carry defense 1–4. Failed infections still soften them by one point, so a siege pays off.
- **Win the dish** — eliminate every red cell, or hold more territory when the round limit hits. Lose all your cells and the run is over.

### 12 dishes, three scene packs
Classic Dish (cyan-white), Resistance Dish (gold-purple), and Mutation Dish (red-black) each change the palette, map shape, and decorations. Later dishes add neutral fortresses, hollow maps, virus storms that mutate random cells, and a smarter AI that looks one step ahead.

### Why it's different
- **The signature moment**: a successful infection bursts into rays of light that chain along the agar, turning a cluster of cells blue at once.
- **Every turn is a choice**: expand now, invest nutrient for next turn, or spend your charged phage to crack a fortified line.
- **Territory with teeth**: defense levels and round limits make every border a small arithmetic gamble.

Free to play, no download. Works with mouse and touch on desktop and mobile. English and Chinese.
