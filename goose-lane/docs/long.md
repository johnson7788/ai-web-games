**GOOSE LANE** is a free endless-lane arcade runner that plays right in your browser. You are a commuter goose squeezed onto the morning metro — the road scrolls forward automatically, and your only job is to stay alive across 12 stations.

You run on a three-lane road through three shifts of the city: the warm-white **Morning Rush**, the teal **Afternoon Shift**, and the blue-purple neon **Night Line**. Every station swaps the hazards, and every chapter changes the whole palette.

### How it plays
- **Switch lanes** — press ←/→ or A/D, tap the left/right half of the screen, or swipe. Timing your lane changes keeps you out of the cars.
- **Grab gems** — ride over them to score and chase a high distance record.
- **Collect balloons** — each balloon absorbs one crash (stack up to 2). Spend them to gamble for gem lines, or save them as a shield.
- **Stop at red gates** — hold Space (or the STOP button) to brake for 1 second and let a red gate turn green; green gates you can blast straight through.
- **Ride the smooth transfer** — chain 3 lane changes within half a second and a white dove flies across the screen for a bonus. That is the signature moment.
- **Watch for hazards** — cars, pits, two-lane narrows, oncoming delivery scooters, and gate mazes all rotate in as you progress.

### Why it's different
- **Balloons are a real decision**: stack them for safety or burn one to grab a gem line in the danger lane.
- **Gates are rhythm breakers**: a red light forces you to stop and wait — the only time the goose stands still.
- **Speed keeps climbing** (8 → 16 m/s) while the road gets tighter, so the same decision gets harder every minute.

Free to play, no download. Works on desktop and mobile, English and Chinese.
