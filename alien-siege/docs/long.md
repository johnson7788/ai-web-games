# ALIEN SIEGE

Alien bugs pour out of the purple fog of a colonial alley. You are the last gun between the colony and the hive. **Move, shoot, reload, and survive 12 escalating waves** across three distinct zones — the purple-gray Colonial Alley, the cyan-green Reaction Field, and the deep-blue Night Sky Port.

The core loop is a fast, readable pseudo-3D raycasting shooter. Aim with your mouse (or the right thumb), move with WASD (or the left joystick), and click to fire. Four weapons unlock as the waves climb: **Pistol → Rifle → Shotgun → Laser**.

Two signature tools change every fight:

- **Laser Mark (E)** — tag an enemy so it takes **1.5× damage**. Mark the fast crawler before it reaches you, or focus down the thick behemoth.
- **Shield Backpack (Q)** — hold to raise an energy shield that absorbs damage, at the cost of **40% slower movement**. Time it against the swarm, or save it for the boss.

Enemy types: fast **Crawlers**, drifting **Floaters**, armored **Behemoths**, and the final **Mother Nest** that endlessly spawns crawlers. Land a close-range shotgun blast to trigger the signature full-screen **purple splatter**.

## 操作说明

- WASD / 左摇杆 — 移动
- 鼠标 / 右半屏拖动 — 转向
- 点击 / 射击按钮 — 射击
- R — 换弹
- E — 镭射标记
- Q — 护盾背包
- Esc — 暂停

## Controls

- WASD / left joystick — move
- Mouse / right-drag — look
- Click / FIRE — shoot
- R — reload
- E — laser mark
- Q — shield
- Esc — pause
