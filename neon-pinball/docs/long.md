**NEON PINBALL** is a free cyber-arcade pinball game that runs in your browser. Charge the plunger, flip neon flippers, and chase the target score across 12 machines — from a pink-purple arcade hall to a cyan server room to an orange-red neon rain street.

You fire a glowing ball onto a table of capacitor bumpers, target lights, ramps, magnets, and black holes. Every hit adds points and builds a combo multiplier; light all target lamps to raise your score multiplier up to ×5.

### How it plays
- **Launch** — hold `SPACE` to charge the plunger, release to fire the ball straight up the table.
- **Flip** — `←`/`Z` left flipper, `→`/`M` right flipper. Tap to catch and relaunch the ball before it drains.
- **Nudge** — `N` gives the table a tilt to save a stuck ball.
- **Touch** — tap the left/right half of the screen to flip.

### The signature moment
Collect all **3 superconductor lamps** and the whole table turns blue-white for 20 seconds: the ball drags an electric trail, every bumper score is ×3, and a fully charged flipper can launch the ball up to the top ring for a bonus.

### Why it's different
- **Charged flippers** — hold a flipper to charge it; a full charge sends the ball to the top ring.
- **Black holes** — they open and close on a timer. Get sucked in and the ball returns 5 seconds later from a random ejector.
- **12 machines** — each one adds a new twist: return ramps, magnet zones, a rotating flipper, double ball, moving targets, dual black holes, and narrow outlanes.

Free to play, no download. Works on desktop and mobile. English and Chinese.
