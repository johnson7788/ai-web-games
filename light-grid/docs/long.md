# LIGHT GRID

**Tap a circuit node to flip it and its four neighbors. Light every node on the board to power the circuit — and beat the fuse.**

LIGHT GRID is a circuit-board maintenance puzzle built on the classic Lights Out rule. Every move flips a node plus the cells around it, so each tap sends a ripple through the board. Finish under the par move count to earn three stars; run out of moves and the fuse blows.

As you advance from 5×5 motherboards to 6×6 graphics cards and 7×7 neon tubes, new circuit elements change the rules: **diodes** only send current downward, **capacitors** need two taps to flip, **relays** fire across the diagonals, and **insulators** can't be tapped directly. The signature moment: when the last node lights up, a surge of current races from the first cell to the last along the board's traces.

**Controls:** tap/click a node (or move with arrow keys / WASD and press Enter/Space). Pause with Esc, restart a level with R, skip the tutorial and replay it from the title screen.

**LIGHT GRID（光网电路）** 是一款「主板检修」主题的点灯解谜游戏：点一个电路节点，会翻转它自己和上下左右相邻的格；把整块板子的节点全部点亮即可通电过关。步数低于本关标准（par）拿三星；步数耗尽保险丝熔断，需要重开。

随着 5×5 主板、6×6 显卡到 7×7 霓虹管三章推进，会逐关引入新格型：**二极管**只向下传导、**电容**要连点两次才翻转、**继电器**联动四个对角、**绝缘格**不可直接点按。签名瞬间：最后一格点亮时，电流从首格沿走线一路奔流到尾格，整块电路被依次唤醒。

**操作**：点按节点即可（也可用方向键 / WASD 移动光标，回车 / 空格确认）；Esc 暂停，R 重开本关；引导可跳过，标题屏可重看。
