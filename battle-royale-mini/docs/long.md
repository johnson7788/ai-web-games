# BATTLE ROYALE MINI

Toy soldiers dropped onto a desk battlefield. Dual-stick move and aim, loot guns, med kits and armor, and outrun the shrinking gas zone. Fifteen AI toys hunt the same loot — gunshots draw them closer, so move silently when you can. Be the last toy standing across 12 ranked drops; the final 1v1 happens under a desk lamp spotlight.

## Controls

- **Desktop**: WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold left mouse / Space to fire, R to reload, Q to use a med kit. Esc to pause.
- **Touch**: left stick to move, right stick to aim, hold FIRE to shoot. (Reload and meds trigger automatically when needed.)

## 迷你吃鸡 · 玩具战场

缩小版玩具兵落进桌面战场：文具和书堆当掩体。双摇杆移动与射击，捡枪、捡医疗包、捡护甲——背包只有 2 格，捡医疗就要丢护甲。缩圈外会持续掉血，枪声会引来 AI。不射击时移速 +30% 且不暴露位置（静步）。12 场排位逐场提高 AI 强度与毒速，名次积分决定最终结算；决赛圈 1v1 时台灯从天打下聚光，是这局最难忘的画面。

## Tips

- Drop near the bright **SAFE ZONE** marker, then grab one of the three starter guns around the center.
- Armor cuts incoming damage 20%; med kits heal 40 HP. A 2-slot backpack forces the trade-off every run.
- The white dashed ring is the next safe zone — get inside before it shrinks.
