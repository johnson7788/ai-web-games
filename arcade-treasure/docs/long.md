# ARCADE TREASURE

ARCADE TREASURE is a retro neon arcade collection. You walk in with a small coin purse and three cabinets to master:

- **Server Night Watch** — whack the gremlins popping out of the server racks, but skip the sparking hazards.
- **Rain Scavenger** — slide the basket to catch falling coins and dodge lightning.
- **Pixel Swarm** — fly your ship left and right and shoot down the descending bee swarm.

Every cabinet costs 1 token to play. Your score cashes back into tokens, so a good run pays for itself. Keep playing the same cabinet to build **heat** and multiply your cash-out up to ×1.5 — specialize or spread your tokens across all three. Clear all three machines to climb the 12 arcade ranks, and when you conquer the final rank, the **GRAND PRIX** neon sign lights up.

**Controls:** tap a cabinet to play. In the machines, tap / move the mouse to aim, tap to hit; on desktop, use ← → and Space to move and shoot in the bee swarm. Esc pauses. Touch-friendly on mobile.

《ARCADE TREASURE》是一款复古霓虹街机合集。你带着一袋代币进入街机厅，挑战三台机台：机房守夜（打地鼠）、雨中拾荒（接金币）、像素蜂群（小蜜蜂）。每台门票 1 枚代币，成绩能换回代币——打得好就白嫖；同台连玩叠热度，最多 ×1.5 倍兑换。三台全部通关即可晋升 12 个厅段位，最终点亮 GRAND PRIX 霓虹招牌。点选机台开玩；机台内移动鼠标/点按瞄准，蜂群机用方向键 + 空格移动射击，Esc 暂停，触屏全兼容。
