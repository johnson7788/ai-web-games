TITLE
ARCADE TREASURE

CATEGORY
Arcade

TAGS
arcade, retro, whack-a-mole, catch coins, space invaders, neon, token economy, mini games

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Play three neon arcade cabinets with one coin purse. Whack gremlins, catch coins, and shoot the bee swarm to light the GRAND PRIX sign.

LONG DESCRIPTION
ARCADE TREASURE is a retro neon arcade collection. You walk in with a small coin purse and three cabinets to master: Server Night Watch (whack the gremlins), Rain Scavenger (catch falling coins), and Pixel Swarm (shoot the descending bee swarm). Every cabinet costs 1 token to play; your score cashes back into tokens so a good run pays for itself. Keep playing the same cabinet to build heat and multiply your cash-out up to ×1.5. Clear all three machines to climb 12 arcade ranks and light the GRAND PRIX neon sign. Tap a cabinet to play, move the mouse to aim, and tap to hit. Touch-friendly.
