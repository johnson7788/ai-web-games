# CAKE KART — Fondant Grand Prix

CAKE KART is a sweet pseudo-3D kart racer. Race a 3-lap loop across a 12-station Grand Prix through three dessert worlds — pink-white Fondant Plains, brown-gold Chocolate Valley and cyan-white Ice Cream Peak. Jelly strips give a speed surge, chocolate-mud zones drag you down, and narrow bridges punish sloppy lines.

**Frosting drift** is the signature: hold Shift while turning to charge a three-tier drift — the fuller the charge, the faster the exit boost, but the harder you slide. Release to fire a cream-tail boost out of the corner. Every track is different (sharp hairpins, long straights, mud zones, narrow bridges), and AI rivals get faster with every station.

**Sweet weapons** drop from item boxes: cherry missiles, wafer shields, jelly boosts, and cake bombs that splatter your rival's screen for 2 seconds — and the AI will blind you right back.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard |
| --- | --- |
| Steer | ← → (or A / D) |
| Throttle | ↑ (or W) |
| Drift | hold Shift while turning |
| Use item | Space |
| Pause | Esc |
| Race again | R (after finish) |

On touch devices, on-screen buttons appear for steering, throttle, drift and items.

# 蛋糕飞车 — 翻糖大奖赛

CAKE KART 是一款甜品卡丁车伪 3D 竞速游戏：12 站翻糖大奖赛、每站三圈，横跨翻糖平原（粉白）、巧克力谷（棕金）、冰淇淋峰（青白）三个场景包。果冻加速带让你冲得更快，巧克力泥减速区拖慢车流，窄桥考验你的走线。

**糖霜漂移**是招牌：转向时按住 Shift 蓄力三档漂移——蓄得越满，出弯加速越猛，但也越难控。松开瞬间车尾喷出奶油拖尾冲进出弯。**甜点武器**从道具箱掉落：樱桃导弹、威化护盾、果冻加速，还有能糊住对手屏幕 2 秒的蛋糕炸弹（AI 也会糊你）。

## 操作

| 动作 | 键盘 |
| --- | --- |
| 转向 | ← →（或 A / D） |
| 油门 | ↑（或 W） |
| 漂移 | 转向时按住 Shift |
| 使用道具 | 空格 |
| 暂停 | Esc |
| 再来一局 | R（完赛后） |

触屏设备会自动显示转向、油门、漂移、道具按钮。
