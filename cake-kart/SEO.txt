SHORT DESCRIPTION
CAKE KART — frosting-drift dessert kart racing. 12-station Grand Prix, 3 laps, sweet weapons, AI rivals, cream drift tail.
蛋糕飞车 — 糖霜漂移甜品卡丁车竞速：12 站大奖赛、3 圈、甜点武器、AI 对手、奶油漂移拖尾。

LONG DESCRIPTION
Race a 3-lap loop across a 12-station Grand Prix through three dessert worlds. Hold Shift while turning to charge a three-tier frosting drift and release a cream-tail boost. Fire cherry missiles, wafer shields, jelly boosts and cake bombs that blind rivals. Jelly strips boost you, chocolate-mud zones slow you, narrow bridges punish bad lines.
三圈竞速穿越 12 站翻糖大奖赛与三个甜品世界。转向时按住 Shift 蓄力三档糖霜漂移，松手喷出奶油拖尾加速。樱桃导弹、威化护盾、果冻加速、糊屏蛋糕炸弹，果冻带加速、巧克力泥减速、窄桥惩罚走线失误。
