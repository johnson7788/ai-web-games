TITLE
EGG BOOM — Kitchen Dough Cracker

CATEGORY
Arcade

TAGS
arcade, runner, shooter, egg, kitchen, casual, one-button, combo

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Roll a springy egg, shoot rice bullets to crack numbered dough balls, and chain combos into a yolk splash.

LONG DESCRIPTION
EGG BOOM is a one-button arcade runner. Hold to charge your jump, release to leap, tap to fire extra rice shots, and crack every numbered dough ball before it reaches you. Chain 5 breaks to charge a yolk splash that cracks armored dough. 12 levels across Morning Kitchen, Dessert Station and Midnight Canteen.
