# EGG BOOM — Kitchen Dough Cracker

EGG BOOM is a fast one-button arcade runner. You are a springy egg rolling across the kitchen counter, firing rice bullets at numbered dough balls that roll toward you. Every shot chips one point off a dough ball's number; clear the whole row before it reaches you, or it cracks your shell.

**How to play**

- **Hold** to charge your spring, **release** to jump. A quick **tap** fires an extra rice shot.
- Your egg **auto-shoots** the lane it is in — so position yourself by jumping to the right height, and decide: jump over a small dough ball to save ammo, or stop and shoot the thick one.
- Every level gives you **one shell** that absorbs a single hit; after it breaks, a new shell grows back in 10 seconds.
- Chain **5 dough breaks in a row** to charge a **yolk splash** — your next tap fires a wide area shot, the only thing that cracks **armored dough** (level 6+).
- Clear a full row to trigger the signature **shell-confetti burst**, plus bonus score and ammo.

**Content**

- 12 levels across 3 scene packs: Morning Kitchen (warm white), Dessert Station (pink), Midnight Canteen (indigo).
- Rotating tricks: thick dough, floating racks, wiggling dough, double-row gaps, and armored dough.
- Three pickups: score stars, ammo rice bags, and firepower flames.

**Controls**

- Desktop: hold **Space** to charge/jump, **F** or **J** to extra-shoot, **Esc/P** pause, **R** restart.
- Touch: hold the screen to charge, release to jump, quick tap to shoot.

# EGG BOOM — 厨房面团爆破

EGG BOOM 是一款快节奏单键街机跑酷。你是一颗滚过厨房台面的弹簧蛋，用米粒弹打碎迎面滚来的数字面粉团：每一发把数字打掉 1 点，必须在它撞到你之前清掉一整排，否则蛋壳会被撞碎。

**怎么玩**

- **按住**蓄力，**松开**起跳；快速**点按**发射一颗额外米粒弹。
- 蛋会自动射击它所在的那一行——跳对高度就是瞄准；决定：跳过小面团省弹药，还是停下来打掉厚面团。
- 每关自带**一层蛋壳**，可抵挡一次撞击；撞碎后 10 秒重新长回。
- 连续打碎 5 个面团就能攒出**蛋黄溅射**——下一发手动弹变成范围弹，也是唯一能打碎**护甲团**（第 6 关起）的弹。
- 清完一整排会触发招牌的**蛋壳彩带爆裂**，并奖励分数与弹药。

**内容**

- 12 关、3 套场景包：清晨厨房（暖白）/ 甜品站（粉）/ 午夜食堂（靛蓝）。
- 关卡轮换特性：厚血团、悬空排、移动团、双排夹缝、护甲团。
- 三种道具：分数星星、弹药米袋、火力火焰。

**操作**

- 桌面：按住 **空格** 蓄力跳，**F / J** 加射，**Esc/P** 暂停，**R** 重开。
- 触屏：按住屏幕蓄力，松开起跳，快速点按加射。
