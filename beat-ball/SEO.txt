TAGS

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Roll a taiko drum ball down a giant drum, dodge beat-synced drumsticks and hit glowing beat pads to chain perfects.

LONG DESCRIPTION
BEAT BALL is a free rhythm-dodge arcade game about rolling a taiko drum ball across a giant festival drum.

The beat drives everything: drumsticks fall exactly on the pulse, glowing beat pads land on the lanes, and every hit you land ripples the drum skin with a taiko crack. Switch lanes with the arrow keys, jump with space, and chain Perfect combos by standing on the beat pads while dodging the sticks that crash down around you.

12 handcrafted stages across three worlds — Dawn Shrine, Lantern Night and Thunder Stage. Each level introduces a new twist: single rail to triple rails, double drumsticks, jump pads, mid-level tempo shifts, fog, cross sticks, and a final Twin Drums finale. Distance is your score; one wrong lane and it is game over.

Easy to pick up, hard to master. Works with keyboard, mouse, and touch on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.

《鼓面疾行》是一款免费的节奏躲避街机游戏：一颗太鼓球在巨大的祭典太鼓面上向纵深滚动。

节拍驱动一切：鼓槌精确落在拍点上，发光节拍点落在轨道上，每次踩拍都会让鼓面泛起涟漪、敲响太鼓。用方向键左右变道、空格跳跃，站在节拍点上连踩 Perfect，同时躲开从天而降的鼓槌。

12 个手工关卡横跨三大场景：晨光祭道、灯笼夜祭、雷雨祭台。每关都有新机制——单轨到三轨、双槌交替、跳板段、中段变速、迷雾、左右开弓，以及终章「双鼓对撞」。距离即分数，走错一条轨道即判负。

十秒上手，越玩越上瘾。键盘、鼠标、触屏全支持，桌面与手机均可游玩。无需下载，浏览器免费畅玩。
