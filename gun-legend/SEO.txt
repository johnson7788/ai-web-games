TITLE
GUN LEGEND — Neon Alley Twin-Gun Shooter

CATEGORY
Action

TAGS
shooter, twin-stick, neon, arcade, bullet-hell, hong-kong, gun, action, dodge, browser game

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A neon Hong Kong alley shooter: aim, fire twin revolvers, roll through bullet curtains, and empty both guns to trigger the white-dove moment.

LONG DESCRIPTION
GUN LEGEND is a neon-soaked Hong Kong alley gunfight. You are the last gunman in the rain: aim with the pointer, fire twin revolvers, roll through enemy bullet curtains, and reload to the rhythm of the fight.

Aim with the mouse or finger, click or hold to fire. Your two revolvers fire in turns — when one runs dry, the other keeps firing seamlessly. Press Shift to roll-dodge with half a second of invincibility, or press R to reload; click inside the rhythm window to finish 30% faster, but mistime it and you drop the magazine.

Empty both guns at the same instant to trigger the signature white-dove moment: slow-motion, a white dove crossing the alley, a free full reload and a combo boost.

Fight through 12 rounds across three scene packs: the cyan-blue Rainy Alley, the gold-orange Rooftop Dusk and the crimson Restaurant Hall. Enemies rotate from knife rushers and cover gunners to grenadiers, dual-wield elites and the Boss. Chain kills to build a combo multiplier. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile.

SHORT DESCRIPTION
霓虹港片巷战射击：指针瞄准、双枪轮流连火、翻滚闪避弹幕，双枪同时清空触发白鸽飞过的吴宇森时刻。

LONG DESCRIPTION
《枪神传说》是一款霓虹港片巷战射击游戏。你是雨夜巷口最后的双枪枪神：指针瞄准、双枪轮流连火、翻滚闪避弹幕，踩着节奏换弹。

移动鼠标或手指瞄准，点击或长按开火。双枪轮流开火，一把打空会自动切到另一把无缝连火。按 Shift 翻滚闪避，获得 0.5 秒无敌；按 R 换弹，在节奏窗口内点击可提前 30% 完成，乱点则打空弹匣重来。

双枪同时清空的瞬间触发标志性的白鸽时刻：慢动作、一只白鸽飞过巷口、双枪免费满膛并获得连杀加成。

在 12 场战斗里穿过三张场景：青蓝霓虹的雨巷、金橙夕照的天台、朱红灯笼的酒楼。敌人从刀手冲锋、枪手掩体到投弹手、双持精英与 Boss 大哥轮番登场。连续击杀积累连杀倍率。支持鼠标、触屏与键盘，桌面与手机均可畅玩。
