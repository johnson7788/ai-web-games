# GUN LEGEND · Neon Alley Shooter

GUN LEGEND is a neon-soaked Hong Kong alley gunfight. You are the last gunman in the rain: aim with the pointer, fire twin revolvers, roll through enemy bullet curtains, and reload to the rhythm of the fight.

## How to play

- **Aim** — move the mouse / finger to steer the white crosshair.
- **Fire** — click or hold to shoot. Your two revolvers fire in turns: when one runs dry, the other keeps firing seamlessly.
- **Roll (Shift / roll button)** — a trench-coat dodge with 0.5s of invincibility (3s cooldown). Time it through grenade blasts and bullet volleys.
- **Reload (R)** — refill both guns. During a reload, click inside the rhythm window to finish 30% faster; mistime it and you drop the magazine.
- **White-dove moment** — empty both guns at the same instant to trigger slow-motion, a white dove crossing the alley, a free full reload, and a combo boost.

Fight through 12 rounds across three scene packs: the cyan-blue Rainy Alley, the gold-orange Rooftop Dusk, and the crimson Restaurant Hall. Enemies rotate from knife rushers and cover gunners to grenadiers, dual-wield elites, and the Boss. Chain kills to build a combo multiplier.

# 枪神传说 · 霓虹巷战

《枪神传说》是一款霓虹港片巷战射击游戏。你是在雨夜巷口最后的双枪枪神：指针瞄准、双枪轮流连火、翻滚闪避弹幕，踩着节奏换弹。

## 操作

- **瞄准**：移动鼠标 / 手指，控制白色十字准星。
- **开火**：点击或长按射击。双枪轮流开火，一把打空会自动切到另一把无缝连火。
- **翻滚（Shift / 触屏按钮）**：风衣闪避，0.5 秒无敌，冷却 3 秒。用来穿过手雷爆炸与子弹齐射。
- **换弹（R）**：补满双枪。换弹时在节奏窗口内点击可提前 30% 完成；乱点会打空弹匣重来。
- **白鸽时刻**：双枪同时清空的瞬间触发慢动作，一只白鸽飞过巷口，双枪免费满膛并获得连杀加成。

在 12 场战斗里穿过三张场景：青蓝霓虹的雨巷、金橙夕照的天台、朱红灯笼的酒楼。敌人从刀手冲锋、枪手掩体到投弹手、双持精英与 Boss 大哥轮番登场。连续击杀积累连杀倍率。
