# BILLION HUSTLE

BILLION HUSTLE is a clicker-tycoon game where you build a startup from a warm little garage all the way to a ¥100,000,000 unicorn. Tap the golden HUSTLE button to earn cash, then spend it on two skill upgrades — Tap Power (each tap earns more) and Auto Income (passive revenue every second) — and hire six partners from Intern up to CMO for even more passive output.

Every 60 seconds the market announces a new trend across six sectors (Live, AI, Meta, Green, Retail, Gaming). Watch the heat bars drift and bet on the sector you think will peak: bet right and you get a 3x income window for 30 seconds. Stack that with Burn Mode — a 2x income sprint for 30 seconds with a 60-second cooldown — for a 6x rush when the timing lines up.

Your lifetime revenue is your **valuation**. Cross each of the 12 funding rounds (Seed → Angel → A → B → … → IPO) and watch the signature **valuation flip** card roll the number and flip your round name. The scene changes as you grow: warm garage, cyan incubator, then a blue-purple skyscraper office. Reach ¥100,000,000 and you become a unicorn.

**Controls:** click/tap the HUSTLE button to earn · click skill/partner buttons to buy · click a sector to bet · click BURN for the sprint · `P`/`Esc` to pause · works with mouse, touch, and `Space` to tap.

《亿万狂飙》是一款点击养成游戏：从暖白车库起步，一路把公司做成估值一亿的独角兽。点击金色 HUSTLE 按钮挣钱，用现金升级「点击力」（每次点击更值钱）与「自动收入」（每秒被动产出），并雇佣从实习生到 CMO 的六位伙伴，获得更强被动收入。

每 60 秒市场会刮起一个风口（直播 / AI / 元宇宙 / 新能源 / 电商 / 游戏六个赛道）。盯着热度条漂移，押注你认为会冲顶的赛道：押中即获得 30 秒 ×3 收益窗口；再叠上「烧钱模式」（30 秒 ×2，冷却 60 秒），时机对上就是 ×6 冲刺。

你的终身营收就是「估值」。跨过 12 个融资轮（种子 → 天使 → A → B → … → IPO），每次融资成功都会触发招牌的「估值翻牌」数字翻滚动画。场景随成长更换：车库暖白、孵化器青、摩天办公蓝紫。估值达到一亿，你就是独角兽。

**操作：** 点击/触摸 HUSTLE 按钮挣钱 · 点击技能/伙伴按钮购买 · 点击赛道押注 · 点击 BURN 开启冲刺 · `P`/`Esc` 暂停 · 支持鼠标、触屏，空格键也能点击。
