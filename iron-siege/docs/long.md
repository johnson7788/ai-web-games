# IRON SIEGE

IRON SIEGE is a steampunk fortress-defense arcade game inspired by classic top-down tank battles. You drive a glowing armored mech at the bottom of a 13×13 grid battlefield and must protect the **Core Furnace** at its center. Enemies roll in from three spawn points at the top — Scouts, fast Raiders, and armored Juggernauts — and they will not stop until the furnace is cold or every wave is cleared.

Your main cannon never runs dry, but it cannot crack **steel walls**. For that you need the limited **heavy shell** — only 2 per wave. Spend them to punch a steel corridor, or save them for the armored tide. You can also **weld-repair** the furnace's brick fence once per wave; the cost is that every enemy on the wave speeds up. Between waves, pick one of two forge upgrades: firepower, traction, core armor, or extra heavy shells.

Clear 12 waves across three visually distinct sectors — the ochre **Iron Wastes**, the blue-white **Snowfield**, and the dark-red **Molten City** — and the fortress stands.

## Controls

- Move: **WASD** or **arrow keys** (touch: left virtual joystick)
- Fire main cannon: **Space** or **J** (touch: FIRE button)
- Fire heavy shell: **K** (touch: HEAVY button)
- Weld-repair: **E** (touch: WELD button)
- Pause: **Esc** or **P** · Restart after defeat: **R**

## 中文说明

《IRON SIEGE》是一款蒸汽朋克要塞防守街机游戏，致敬经典俯视角坦克大战。你驾驶发光的装甲机甲，守在 13×13 战场的底部，保卫正中央的「核心熔炉」。敌人从顶部三处出生点涌入——侦察兵、快速突袭者与重装机甲——直到熔炉冷却或清完所有波次才会停下。

主炮弹药无限，但打不穿**钢墙**；只有每波 2 发的限量**重炮弹**能击穿钢墙。是凿开钢墙走廊，还是留给重甲潮，全看你的选择。每关还可**焊修**一次熔炉砖墙，代价是本波敌人全部提速。波间二选一熔炉强化：火力 / 牵引速度 / 核心护甲 / 重炮弹补给。

横跨三张风格迥异的战区——土黄的钢铁荒原、蓝白的雪原、暗红的熔城——清完 12 波，要塞屹立不倒。

## 操作

- 移动：**WASD** 或 **方向键**（触屏：左下摇杆）
- 主炮：**空格** 或 **J**（触屏：开火键）
- 重炮：**K**（触屏：重炮键）
- 焊修：**E**（触屏：焊修键）
- 暂停：**Esc** 或 **P** · 失败后重开：**R**
