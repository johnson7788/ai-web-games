# HELIX ORBIT

HELIX ORBIT is a pseudo-3D arcade dropper. You are a meteor core falling through a living volcano helix. Hold **← / →** (or drag on touch) to rotate the tower so a gap lines up under the ball — it drops through to the next layer. Land on a magma segment and it is game over.

Chain **3 passes in a row** to enter **Meteor state** and smash straight through magma with a lava burst. Collect **ice crystals** on the way down; fill the meter to **freeze all magma for 5 seconds**. Every 5th layer hides a **core crevice** — a narrower gap that doubles your score. Three scene packs change the look as you descend: the ochre **Crust**, the black-and-violet **Obsidian**, and the cyan-white **Ice Cap**. Twelve difficulty stages rotate the rules: double gaps, reverse spin, drifting gaps, narrow gaps and rising magma.

HELIX ORBIT 是一款伪 3D 街机下落游戏。你是一颗坠向活火山岩芯的流星核心：按住 **← / →**（触屏拖动）旋转螺旋塔，让层间缺口对齐到球的正下方，球就会穿落到下一层；踩上岩浆段则判负。连续穿落 3 层进入**陨石态**，可直接砸穿岩浆、触发熔岩四溅。沿途收集**冰晶**，攒满后**冻结全部岩浆 5 秒**变安全。每 5 层出现一条**岩芯裂隙**——更窄的缝，穿落得双倍分。随着下坠依次经过**岩壳（赭红）/ 黑曜（墨黑紫纹）/ 冰帽（青白）**三套场景，12 个难度段轮换双缺口、反向旋转、移动缝、窄缝与岩浆占比。
