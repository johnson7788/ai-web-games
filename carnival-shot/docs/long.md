# CARNIVAL SHOT

**CARNIVAL SHOT** is a night-market lantern fair shooting gallery. Paper kites and lantern ducks fly across the warm night sky — click or tap them to score. Different targets are worth different points: slow lantern ducks, sliding bullseyes, diving ducks, and a rare timed golden duck. Watch out for the pale ghost lanterns — shooting one costs you 150 points.

Your clip holds only **6 rounds**. Run dry and you reload slowly; press **R** (or tap the reload button) early to bank a **speed clip**, so your next reload is twice as fast. Land consecutive hits to build a combo — reach **×8** and the whole fair erupts in a golden **lantern rain**.

Every level hides **3 red reward ducks**: hit one to spin the wheel for **double points**, **+5 seconds**, or a **full clip**.

**Controls:** Click / tap to shoot · **R** or the on-screen button to reload · **Esc** to pause. Work through **12 levels** across three scenes — the warm-red Lantern Fair, the cyan-blue River Lantern, and the gold-purple Fireworks Night.

**CARNIVAL SHOT**（夜市猎鸭庙会）是一款限时打靶游戏。点击射击横飞的纸鸢与提灯鸭，不同目标分值不同；小心小鬼灯，打中会扣 150 分。弹匣只有 6 发，打空自动换弹较慢——按 **R** 提前换弹可装「速装匣」，让下一次换弹减半。连续命中积累连击，达到 **×8** 会触发全场金光「孔明灯雨」。每关有 3 只红灯鸭，打中即可转动轮盘：双倍积分、+5 秒、或弹匣填满。

**操作：** 点击 / 触摸射击 · **R** 或屏幕按钮换弹 · **Esc** 暂停。共 **12 关**，横跨灯会（暖红）、河灯（青蓝）、烟花夜（金紫）三套场景。
