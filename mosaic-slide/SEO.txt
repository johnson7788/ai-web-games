SHORT DESCRIPTION
Slide colorful stained-glass shards into the gap to rebuild a glowing rose window before the sun sets.

LONG DESCRIPTION
MOSAIC SLIDE is a free sliding-tile puzzle game set in a stained-glass workshop. Tap a glass shard next to the dark gap and slide it into place, one shimmering piece at a time, until the shattered window becomes a perfect symmetric rose.

Every level generates a unique hand-crafted-feeling stained-glass window from a seeded procedural pattern — geometric mosaics, blooming rosettes and candlelit night windows. Rebuild it, and the finishing move floods the whole window with sunlight: the signature moment every player remembers.

12 levels across three scenes: Workshop Dawn, Glass Afternoon and Candle Night. Start with a gentle 3×3, then master hexagon cuts, timed sprints, rotating shards, memory puzzles where the goal image hides after five seconds, and dual interwoven patterns before the grand 6×6 finale.

Three stars per level: finish the window, finish within the move goal, and finish fast enough for the time star. Track your best stars across all 12 windows.

Easy to learn in ten seconds, hard to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.
