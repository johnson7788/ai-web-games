TITLE
Launch Legends — Free Firework Climbing Arcade Game

CATEGORY
Arcade

TAGS
fireworks, rocket, climb, arcade, one-button, casual, night sky, reflex, browser game, family friendly

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Steer a bouncing firework shell into the night sky: smash cloud wisps to score and charge your fuse, then burn through steel bars.

LONG DESCRIPTION
LAUNCH LEGENDS is a free vertical-climbing arcade game about one firework shell chasing the summit of the night sky.

The shell bounces on its own — you only steer left and right. Every cloud wisp you smash scores a spark and charges your fuse; fill the fuse and the shell ignites into an incandescent state whose tail flame burns straight through the steel bars that would otherwise end your run. Climb higher and a ring of mini fireworks blooms around you every five meters — your signature in the sky.

12 handcrafted levels across three worlds: Town Night, Above the Clouds and Ring Summit. Crosswinds push you off course, cloud cascades reward perfect combos, steel canopies demand a timed ignition, rain bands slow your bounce, moving decks drift beneath you, and narrow wells leave almost no room for error. Height is your score — hit the target and claim up to three stars.

Crash into steel while cold and your run fizzles out, but a revive can reignite you mid-flight. Easy to learn in five seconds, hard to master. Works with keyboard, mouse and touch on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.

TAGS

SHORT DESCRIPTION
操控一颗自动弹跳的烟花弹冲上夜空：撞碎云絮得分并给引信充能，再烧穿钢棚一路登顶。

LONG DESCRIPTION
《升空传说》是一款免费纵向攀升街机游戏：一颗烟花弹独自追逐夜空的顶点。

烟花弹会自动弹跳，你只需左右操控。撞碎每一片云絮都能得分、充能；蓄满引信后进入炽热态，尾焰能烧穿挡路的钢棚——而冷却时撞上钢棚就会熄火坠毁。每爬升 5 米，弹体周围就绽放一圈迷你烟花，这是你在夜空里留下的签名。

12 个手工关卡横跨三大场景：小城夜空、云海之上、星环之巅。横风把你吹偏，云絮连击奖励完美节奏，钢棚逼你掐秒点火，阵雨带下压减速，移动云台在脚下漂移，窄井通道几乎不留容错。高度就是分数——达到目标高度即可摘下一至三星。

撞上钢棚前没有炽热态就熄火，但一次复活能在半空重新点燃你。五秒上手，越玩越难。键盘、鼠标、触屏全支持，桌面与手机均可游玩。无需下载，浏览器免费畅玩。
