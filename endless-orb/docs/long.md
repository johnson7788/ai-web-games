# ENDLESS ORB

**ENDLESS ORB** is a neon arcade skill game: a huge mechanical star-core spins at the center of the screen, and you tap (or press Space) to fire a photon straight into it. The photon sticks to the core's rim where it lands. Pin the target number of photons to clear a ring — but if your photon touches one already pinned, the core collapses and the run ends.

Every ring changes the rules. The core speeds up, reverses direction, sways side to side, splits into two inter-orbiting cores, and periodically flips magnetic polarity — sucking a pinned photon out and reversing the spin. Every 20th pin is an **overload photon**: twice the size, covering two slots, and far harder to squeeze into a gap. Clear all 12 rings and ride the ripple.

## How to play

- **Tap / click / Space** — fire a photon straight up.
- **Wait for the gap** — time your shot so the landing slot is empty when the photon arrives.
- **Clear the ring** — reach the pin target to trigger the core-pulse ripple and advance.
- **Pick an upgrade** — after each ring, choose one of three rule/stat upgrades to shape your run.

**ENDLESS ORB** 是一款霓虹街机技巧游戏：一颗巨大的机械星核在屏幕中央旋转，你点击（或按空格）把光子直线射进去。光子会钉在它落点的核边缘上；钉满目标数量的光子即可清空一环——但要是光子撞到已经钉上去的光子，星核就会坍缩，本局结束。

每一环都会换规则：星核加速、反向、左右摇摆、分裂成两颗互绕小核，还会周期性磁极反转——吸走一颗已钉光子并反转旋转方向。每 20 钉会来一颗**过载重光子**：两倍大小、占两格位，塞进空档难得多。清空全部 12 环，看脉冲涟漪荡满全屏。

## 操作

- **点击 / 触屏 / 空格** — 发射光子。
- **等空档** — 看准落点空位再出手。
- **清环** — 达到目标钉数触发清屏涟漪并推进。
- **选升级** — 每清一环三选一，打造本局 build。
