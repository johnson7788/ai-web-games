# TYCOON EMPIRE — 1920s Banker Idle Tycoon

Tap the golden tower to earn cash. Spend it to buy 12 industries — from a street newsstand and a laundry, up through diners, radio studios, theaters, newspapers, department stores, auto works, shipping lines, railways and oil trusts, all the way to an international bank. Every business has its own cost and income curve: upgrade its level for geometric growth, and hire a manager to collect its income automatically.

Two risks make it a real game of nerve:

- **Market Day** every 30 seconds — one industry is hit by a rumor: boom ×2 or crash ×0.5. Hit **All In** to bet your entire cash pile on the direction. Win and double your money; miss and lose half.
- **Loans** — borrow 10× your cash to expand fast, but interest of 5% per minute drains your balance. If you can't cover it, you go bankrupt and your industries reset.

Every time an industry's level doubles, a golden chain completes and your tower rises one floor. Your empire shifts from a warm street corner to a golden downtown to a blue-purple finance tower as you climb the 12 industry milestones.

## Controls

- **Tap / click** the tower to earn cash (hold for rapid tapping).
- **Buy / Up / Mgr** buttons buy a business, upgrade it, or hire a manager.
- **Collect** gathers income from businesses that have no manager yet.
- **All In ▲ / ▼** bets all cash on a market boom or crash.
- **Loan / Repay** borrows 10× cash or pays it back.
- **Esc** pauses; language toggle in the top-right.
