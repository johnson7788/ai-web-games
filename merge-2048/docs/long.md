# MERGE 2048 — Star Colony Slide

Slide the whole fleet with arrow keys, WASD, or a swipe. When two ships of equal tonnage touch, they merge into one bigger ship and your score climbs. Reach 32t to clear the first sector, then push on through 12 sectors of rotating rules: narrow 3×4 lanes, debris obstacles, spawn-only-4 runs, wild 2/4/8 drops, and mirrored controls.

Two original twists change how you plan. Every few moves a **gravity well** opens on a random cell for 3 moves — any ship that slides in gets locked and cannot merge, so route around it. And once per sector you have a **warp engine**: pick any two equal ships and force-merge them, even if one is locked. Reach 128t for a greenhouse, 256t for a mine, 512t for an orbital station, 1024t for a stargate, and 2048t to light the warp drive and win.

Three scene packs — Low Earth Orbit (deep blue), the Asteroid Belt (gray-orange), and the Deep Nebula (purple-pink) — repaint the sky as you advance.

滑动舰队，合并吨位，把星际殖民地一路建到 2048。方向键 / WASD / 滑动手势整盘滑动，同吨位舰合并翻倍；每步会生成一艘新舰（2 或 4 吨），无法移动即失败，达成 2048 可继续。

两个原创机制：**引力井**每若干步随机一格，滑进去的舰会被锁定 3 步不可合并，需要规划绕行；**跃迁引擎**每关 1 次，可把任意两个同值格强制合并（包括被锁定的）。12 关推进结构轮换 3×4 窄盘、初始障碍格、只生成 4、狂野 8、镜像盘等特性；达到 128/256/512/1024/2048 吨位逐级解锁温室、矿场、轨道站、星门与曲率引擎。三个场景包：近地轨道（深蓝）、小行星带（灰橙）、星云深处（紫粉）。

操作：方向键 / WASD 滑动；触屏滑动；`E` 键或「跃迁」按钮发动跃迁引擎；`Esc`/`P` 暂停；`R` 重开；标题/暂停可切中英文。
