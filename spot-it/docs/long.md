# SPOT IT

The night flea market is open, and your only light is a flashlight that keeps shrinking. Sweep the beam across crowded book stalls, toy heaps, and the ghost market to find the goods on your checklist. Every correct pick recharges your light and lights up a stall. Every wrong pick costs you time — and in later levels, decoy twins hide a single missing detail that costs double if you fall for them.

**How to play**

- Move your mouse (or drag your finger) to sweep the flashlight.
- Tap an item to pick it up.
- Clear the whole checklist before the timer runs out to light the market and advance.
- Watch your light meter: it drains as you search, and finding items refills it.
- In higher levels, items get smaller and decoy twins appear — slow down and compare the details.

12 levels across three scenes, one signature moment: when you finish a list, the market's lanterns switch on one by one.
