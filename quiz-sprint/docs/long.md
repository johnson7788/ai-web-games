# QUIZ SPRINT — Knowledge Marathon

QUIZ SPRINT is a trivia racing game where your knowledge is your speed. Read a multiple-choice question, tap an answer within the timer, and watch your runner surge down the track — or stumble and slow down when you get it wrong. You race against three AI rivals across a 12-race campaign, with every correct answer building a combo that speeds you up.

**How to play:** Click or tap one of the four answer options before the timer runs out. A correct answer surges you forward and restores stamina; a wrong answer (or running out of time) knocks you back and drains stamina. Chain 5 correct answers to trigger the **Scholar Sprint** — a glowing afterimage surge. Every 5th question is a **DOUBLE wager question**: you see the category first, then choose to answer for double reward/damage or skip for a safe small advance. Manage your stamina bar — when it empties, your pace collapses until it recovers.

**Three scene packs** rotate as you advance: a bright Morning Run, a green Mountain Road, and a neon Night City. Race distances grow, categories rotate (General / Science / Geography / Sports), and the AI gets faster. Reach the finish line first to take 1st place.

**Controls:** Click/tap an option (desktop keys 1–4 also work). Esc/P to pause, R to race again. Works on touch with buttons ≥44px.

《QUIZ SPRINT（答题冲刺）》是一款知识竞速游戏：你的知识就是你的速度。限时读题、四选一作答，答对冲刺前进、答错后退掉体力，与三名 AI 对手在 12 场赛段里争夺名次。连对加速，5 连对触发「学霸冲刺」残影；每 5 题一次**双倍赌注题**——先看类别，再决定「双倍作答」或「跳过求稳」。体力条见底会降速，答对才能回体力。三套场景（晨跑亮色 / 山道绿调 / 夜城霓虹）随章节切换，距离递增、类别轮换、AI 提速。点选选项即可操作（桌面可用 1–4 键），触屏按钮 ≥44px，横屏体验最佳。
