TITLE
MERGE MELON DROP

CATEGORY
Puzzle

TAGS
merge, drop, suika, watermelon, fruit, arcade, casual, physics, neon

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Drop and merge neon fruits into the NOVA melon in this juicy arcade puzzle.

LONG DESCRIPTION
MERGE MELON DROP is a neon arcade merge-drop puzzle. Aim and drop glowing fruits into the jar; two matching fruits fuse into the next tier, all the way to the legendary NOVA melon. Dodge the rocks, chain your merges for combo bonus, and chase the 3-star harvest. Pure one-touch controls, works on desktop and mobile.
