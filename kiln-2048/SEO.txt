TAGS

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Drop clay glaze balls, merge equal values, and fire your way up to the Sky Celadon 2048.

LONG DESCRIPTION
KILN 2048 is a free drop-merge puzzle game set inside a glowing pottery kiln.

Aim your next clay ball and drop it. When two equal glaze balls touch, they merge and double — Bisque becomes Celadon, Celadon becomes Jun Red, and on up the kiln temperatures. Reach 2048 and the legendary Sky Celadon fires out of the kiln in a flash of warm light. But keep the pile below the top fail line, or the kiln overflows and the run is lost.

12 handcrafted levels across three scenes: Morning Workshop, Dusk Kiln and Night Fire. Frozen glaze balls block your board until two firings thaw them, dual-color glaze only merges on alternating turns, big distractor balls throw off your aim, and a limited hammer lets you smash any ball to save a run. The kiln fire brightens as your highest value climbs.

Easy to learn in ten seconds, hard to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.

瞄准下一枚陶土釉球并投放下去。数值相同的釉球相碰就会合成翻倍——素坯变青釉、青釉变钧红，一路升温复烧。炼到 2048，传说中的天青出窑闪光消除。但别让陶土堆过顶部失败线，否则窑满溢出、本局失败。

十秒上手，越玩越烧脑。鼠标、触屏、键盘全支持，桌面与手机均可游玩。无需下载，浏览器免费畅玩。
