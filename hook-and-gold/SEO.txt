TITLE
HOOK & GOLD — Deep Sea Treasure Salvage Game

CATEGORY
Casual

TAGS
treasure hunt, gold miner, deep sea, salvage, claw machine, timing, casual, arcade, browser game, family friendly

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Swing the salvage claw, hook sunken treasure, and haul it back to your boat before the tide runs out.

LONG DESCRIPTION
HOOK & GOLD is a free deep-sea salvage game: pilot the salvage boat and swing a mechanical claw to hook treasure from the ocean floor.

Watch the claw sway, then click or press Space at just the right moment to fire it straight at a target. Heavy hauls reel in slowly — gold ingots pay big, dumbbell scrap pays nothing, black pearls are light and worth a fortune. Jellyfish sting away your time, pearl shells snap shut, and dynamite barrels blow up everything nearby. The water current drifts objects sideways, and the night sea hides them in shadow.

12 handcrafted levels across three worlds: Shallow Reef, Deep Blue Shipwreck and Night Sea. Reach the target haul before the countdown ends, and finish with time to spare to earn three stars. Limited dynamite lets you abandon a slow, bad haul — spend it wisely.

Ten seconds to learn, hard to master. Mouse, touch and keyboard all work on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.

钩金沉海 — 深海打捞寻宝游戏

CATEGORY

TAGS

SHORT DESCRIPTION
摆动机械锚爪，钩起海底沉船宝藏，赶在潮水退去前拖上打捞船。

LONG DESCRIPTION
《钩金沉海》是一款免费的深海打捞游戏：驾驶打捞船，摆动机械锚爪，把沉船宝藏一件件拖上甲板。

看准锚爪摆动，在合适时机点击或按空格直线发射。越重的宝回收越慢——金锭最值钱、废铁哑铃几乎一文不值、黑珍珠轻巧又值钱。水母会蜇人扣时间，珍珠贝会突然闭合，炸药桶会炸掉周边。洋流让物件缓缓横移，夜海月光把宝藏藏进半影里。

12 个手工关卡横跨三大场景：浅礁碧海、深蓝沉船、夜海月光。赶在倒计时结束前凑够目标金额，剩的时间越多、星级越高。每局限量炸药，可以放弃一次慢吞吞的坏钩——省着用。

十秒上手，越玩越上头。鼠标、触屏、键盘全支持，桌面与手机均可游玩。无需下载，浏览器免费畅玩。
