# HEROES QUEST — Tactical Expedition

HEROES QUEST is a turn-based tactical expedition RPG. Lead three heroes — **Kael the Vanguard**, **Syl the Ranger**, and **Nyx the Seer** — across a 12-node branching map of battles, events, rest camps, elite fights, and a final Warlord boss.

Every node you march costs **supplies**. Winning battles seizes more supplies, but costs HP; rest nodes heal your squad but yield little. Choose your route, arrange your **front / mid / back formation** before each fight (the front row absorbs blows, the back row is protected), and manage skill cooldowns on a **speed-bar** turn order. Charge the shared **Expedition Might** gauge, then unleash the three-hero combo to sweep the enemy line.

Three scene packs change the whole look as you advance: the green **Verdant March**, the blue-white **Snowfield Trek**, and the dark-red **Ash Expanse**.

## How to play

- **Map**: click a glowing node to march.
- **Formation**: click a hero, then a row slot, then **Confirm formation**.
- **Battle**: on your turn, click a skill, then click a target (enemy to strike, ally to heal). Click **Expedition Might** when the gauge is full.
- **Keyboard**: `Esc` pause, `R` new quest (on the result screen), `Enter` start.
- **Touch**: the same — tap nodes, skills, and targets.

## 英雄远征

《英雄远征》是一款回合制战术远征 RPG。率领三名英雄——先锋凯尔、游侠希尔、先知妮克丝——踏上 12 节点的分支远征地图：战斗、事件、休整、精英战，直至最终军阀 Boss。

每走一个节点都要消耗**粮草**；战斗胜利能抢粮，休整节点能回血。战前在前/中/后三列**布阵**（前排承伤、后排受保护），战斗中按**速度条**回合行动、管理技能冷却，积攒共享的**远征军势**，三英合击扫清敌阵。随章推进，三套场景依次切换：绿野行军、雪原跋涉、灰烬旷野。

操作：地图上点击发光节点行军；布阵屏点英雄再点阵位、确认；战斗中选技能再选目标（敌方=攻击、友方=治疗），军势满时释放合击。
