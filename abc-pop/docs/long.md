# ABC POP — Pop the Letter Bubbles

**ABC POP** is a gentle, no-fail alphabet game for little learners. Colorful letter balloons float through a candy sky, and your job is to pop the ones that match the target letter shown at the top.

Each of the 26 letters has three rounds:

1. **Find the letter** — spot and pop the uppercase target.
2. **Both forms** — pop the uppercase *and* lowercase forms of the same letter.
3. **First letter** — read a word (Apple 🍎, Cat 🐱 …) and pop its first letter.

Wrong taps never cost points — the bubble just wiggles and your streak resets. Pop 5 in a row to light up a **rainbow letter bridge**. As you advance, the wind speeds up, more lookalike letters sneak in, and the bubbles start crossing each other, so tracking the target gets trickier.

The sky changes as you progress: **Morning Clouds** (A–I), **After the Rain** (J–R), and **Night Sky** (S–Z).

## How to play

- **Desktop**: click/tap the letter bubbles with the mouse.
- **Mobile / touch**: tap bubbles directly — buttons are touch-friendly.
- **Pause**: press `Esc` (or `P`).
- **Language**: switch between English / 中文 in the top-right menu or Settings.

# ABC POP — 点破字母泡泡

**ABC POP** 是一款无挫败的幼儿字母认知游戏。彩色字母气球在糖果天空中飘来飘去，你的任务是点破与顶部目标字母相同的泡泡。

26 个字母，每个字母三轮：

1. **找字母**——找到并点破目标大写字母。
2. **大小写**——同一字母的大写和小写各点一次。
3. **首字母**——读一个单词（Apple 🍎、Cat 🐱 …），点它的首字母。

点错不扣分，泡泡只会抖一抖并打断连击；连续点对 5 个会点亮一道**彩虹字母桥**。越往后风速越快、形近字母越多、泡泡还会交叉换位，追踪目标越来越有挑战。

场景随进度切换：**晨云**（A–I）、**雨后**（J–R）、**夜空**（S–Z）。

## 操作说明

- **电脑**：鼠标点击字母泡泡。
- **手机 / 触屏**：直接点泡泡，按钮支持触控。
- **暂停**：按 `Esc` 或 `P`。
- **语言**：右上角下拉或设置中切换 English / 中文。
