TITLE
DICE CASCADE

CATEGORY
Puzzle

TAGS
dice, pachinko, rune, temple, betting, push your luck, probability, casual

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Bet rune dice, cast them down a temple pachinko board, and bank your score before cursed faces wipe it. Triple runes open the golden door.

LONG DESCRIPTION
Cast rune dice into a temple pachinko board. Each die face picks a lane and a base value; beads bounce through pegs into multiplier buckets. Bet 1-3 die slots for x2/x3, manage expectation, bank your score before cursed faces wipe it, and collect runes for permanent cross-run upgrades across the Stone, Jade and Lava temples.
