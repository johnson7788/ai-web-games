# DICE CASCADE — Rune Dice Temple

Cast rune dice into a temple pachinko board. Each die face decides which lane a bead drops into and how much it is worth; beads bounce through the peg field and land in multiplier buckets at the bottom. Before you roll, you may **bet** on 1–3 die slots — hit one for ×2, hit two or more for ×3. Every bet costs unbanked score, so you are always managing expectation.

Each temple stage has a **target score** and a limited number of rolls. When you reach the target, press **BANK** to lock your points and move on — or keep rolling to chase a bigger score and risk having a cursed face wipe the pot. From stage 4, the face "1" becomes a **cursed face** (negative points); later stages add heavier curses, a **lucky wheel**, and tighter roll counts across three temple themes: the Stone Temple (sand gold), the Jade Temple (green), and the Lava Temple (red black). Rolling three matching runes opens the golden door for a signature bonus.

**Controls:** click/tap a die slot to bet, press **ROLL** to cast, press **BANK** to lock your score. `Esc` pauses, `R` restarts after a run.

**Progression:** runes collected from the center bucket fill a permanent rune meter. Each level grants a small permanent bonus (an extra roll, a bet rebate, a cursed ward, a stronger start, and more) that carries across every run.

在概率神殿里掷符文骰，骰面决定弹珠落区与基础倍率，弹珠穿过钉阵掉进底部的倍率桶计分。掷骰前可**押注** 1–3 个骰位：押中 1 个 ×2、押中 2 个以上 ×3；押注会消耗未锁定分数，你需要管理期望。每关有**目标分**和有限投掷次数，达到目标后点**收手保分**锁定分数并进入下一关，也可以继续掷冲高分，但第 4 关起骰面 1 变为**诅咒面（负点）**，可能清空未锁定分数。后续还有幸运轮、更重诅咒与更紧的投掷次数，并依次切换**石殿（沙金）/ 翡翠殿（青绿）/ 熔岩殿（红黑）**三套场景。三骰同面会触发三连符文，神殿门金光洞开。

**操作**：点击/点按骰位下注，点**掷骰**投出符文骰，点**收手保分**锁定分数。`Esc` 暂停，`R` 结算后重开。

**成长**：中央符文桶收集的符文会填满永久符文槽，每升一级获得跨局永久小加成（额外投掷、押注返还、诅咒抵消、开局底分等）。
