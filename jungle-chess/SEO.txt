LONG DESCRIPTION
JUNGLE CHESS is a free animal-chess board game about commanding a jungle beast troop across an emerald rainforest map.

Eight ranks of beasts face off: elephant, lion, tiger, leopard, wolf, dog, cat and rat. The strong devour the weak — but the tiny rat can take down the elephant, the one great exception. Only the rat may swim the river, while tiger and lion leap straight across it in a single bound. Lure enemies into your traps and any of your beasts can finish them. Reach the rival den to win.

Twelve handcrafted levels build the rules one lesson at a time: six quick checkmate-style puzzles, then six full battles against three AI rivals — the Attendant, the Senior and the Elder, ending with the Elder's Secret at perfect difficulty. Cute beast-head badges are shape-and-color coded so both sides are readable at a glance.

Every capture erupts in a tangle of jungle vines, and a winning den triggers a homecoming parade of your surviving beasts. Three scenes — Dawn Mist, Monsoon Forest and Night Song — repaint the whole board as you advance.

Easy to learn in seconds, tricky to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.
