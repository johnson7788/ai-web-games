# Q ADVENTURE

You are a jelly-bean knight bouncing across three dream worlds — Candy Hills, Cloud Walk and the Night Forest — to save the princess. Run, double-jump, wall-cling and wall-jump; stomp patrolling bots to bounce higher, grab every star and coin, and reach the flag at the end of each level.

The jelly has rhythm: jump on the beat of the background music for a +15% height boost that lets you reach hidden routes and clear spike pits. The safest path has few stars; the risky lower path is full of them. Enemies behave like a real behavior tree — they patrol until they spot you, then give chase.

**Controls:** ←/→ or A/D to run · Space to jump (press again in mid-air to double-jump, or push into a wall and jump to wall-jump) · R to retry. On touch devices, use the on-screen ◀ ▶ and jump buttons.

你就是一颗要救公主的果冻豆骑士，在糖果丘、云端与夜林三个世界横版冒险。跑、二段跳、贴墙吸附再蹬墙跳，踩扁巡逻机器人借力弹得更高；收集星星与金币，抵达每关终点的旗帜。果冻有节奏：踩着背景音乐的鼓点起跳，跳跃高度 +15%，能摸到隐藏路线、越过尖刺阵。上路安全但星星少，下路危险却星星多。敌人像行为树一样思考——平时巡逻，发现你就追击。

**操作：** ←/→ 或 A/D 奔跑 · 空格跳跃（空中再按一次二段跳，贴墙时按跳为蹬墙跳）· R 重开。触屏设备使用屏幕上的 ◀ ▶ 与跳跃按钮。
