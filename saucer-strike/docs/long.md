# SAUCER STRIKE

A retro radar-station defense game. You man a magnetic railgun at the bottom of the screen. Press and drag to aim and charge, release to fire, and intercept waves of UFOs before they slip past your radar. Every saucer you destroy scores points by type; every saucer that escapes costs you a life.

**How to play**
- **Aim & charge** — press and drag; the farther you drag, the more power (and the more damage).
- **Fire** — release to launch a plasma bolt.
- **Overheat** — firing at near-full charge locks the railgun for 2 seconds. Low-charge rapid fire is safe; full-charge shots hit hard but run hot.
- **Tracer** — once per wave, fire a tracer flare to reveal cloaked saucers and their flight paths for 3 seconds. Save it for the invisible waves.

**The threat**
- **Scout** — fast and fragile (100 pts).
- **Transport** — thick armor, needs several hits (300 pts).
- **Z-saucer** — zig-zag flight path (200 pts).
- **Cloaked saucer** — invisible except for brief flickers (400 pts).
- **Mother saucer** — a heavy escort leader (1000 pts). A full-charge hit tears a beam across the sky and flashes the radar grid red.

**Progression** — 12 waves across three sectors: the Desert Radar, the Aurora Base, and the City Skyline. Meteor showers in some waves block your shots. Between waves, pick one of three upgrades to build a different defense each run. Finish all 12 waves to clear the sector.

SAUCER STRIKE 是一款复古雷达站守卫战。你操控屏幕底部的磁轨炮：按住拖动来瞄准蓄力，松开发射，拦截一波波 UFO。命中按碟型计分，漏碟扣命。满蓄力发射会过热锁炮 2 秒——低蓄速射安全，高蓄高伤但危险。每波有 1 发曳光弹，命中后 3 秒内所有飞碟（含隐形碟）显形、轨迹可预判。12 波横跨荒漠雷达站、极光基地、城市天际线三个战区；部分波次有流星雨挡弹；波次之间三选一升级。守满 12 波即通关。
