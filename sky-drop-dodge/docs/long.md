# SKY DROP DODGE — long description

Run across a city rooftop under a sky that never stops dropping things. Flowerpots, bottles, fridges, sofas and even homing drones fall toward you in 12 escalating waves — from a calm flowerpot drizzle to a stormy downpour, a sneak-attack wave with almost no warning, and a final all-out finale. Every run crosses three hand-drawn rooftops: a bright blue noon, a gray-purple thunderstorm, and a gold-orange dusk.

Your two tools are simple but force real choices. **Raise your energy umbrella** (Space / the on-screen button) to block small objects — each block costs one of three shield charges, and blocking a big fridge shatters the umbrella entirely. Spend your shield on small junk to stay safe, or save it for the heavy drop. **Ride the wind lanes** painted on the ground: tailwind speeds you up, headwind slows you down. Borrow the wind to dodge, but don't get pushed into a falling fridge.

Dodge close — really close — and you earn style points. Chain two near-misses and the world slips into a **slow-motion ripple**, giving you a breath to weave through the rain. Survive all 12 waves to clear the sky. How far and how stylish can you go?

## 中文简介

在城市天台顶奔跑，头顶的天空永远有东西往下掉——花盆、水瓶、冰箱、沙发，甚至还会追踪你的无人机。12 波越下越猛：从稀疏的花盆雨，到倾盆暴雨、几乎没有预警的偷袭波，再到最后的全要素混战。每局跨越三个手绘天台：亮蓝的晴午、灰紫电光的雷雨、金橙的晚霞。

两件工具带来真正的取舍。**举起能量伞**（空格 / 屏幕按钮）可以挡下小物件，每次格挡消耗三格能量中的一格；用伞硬接大冰箱会直接碎伞。是把盾留给小物保平安，还是留到重物砸下的那一刻？**借地面风带走位**：顺风加速、逆风减速，抢到顺风能更快躲开，但也可能被风推向墙边或坠物。

擦着坠物边躲过会积累「风骚值」；连续两次擦身，世界会进入**慢动作涟漪**，让你在雨点般的坠物间穿针引线。撑过全部 12 波，天空就会放晴。你能走多远、能有多风骚？

## Controls / 操作

- **← / →** or **A / D**: move left / right（左右移动）
- **Space**: raise umbrella（举伞格挡）
- **Esc / P**: pause（暂停）
- **R**: restart after a run（结算后重开）
- **Touch**: drag anywhere to move, hold the ☂ button to raise your umbrella（触屏：拖动移动，按住 ☂ 按钮举伞）
