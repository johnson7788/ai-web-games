# DONKEY HURDLE — Silk Road Runner

You are a little donkey hauling a caravan of terracotta jars across the Silk Road. The donkey runs on its own; you time the jumps. Every jar is your health — hit a hurdle and a jar shatters off your back. Run out of jars and the caravan falls apart.

**Controls**

- Tap / click / **Space** — jump (a quick tap = short hop).
- **Hold** the jump — jump higher to clear tall walls.
- **K** (or the on-screen K button on touch) — drop a jar for a super-high jump.

**Depth**

- **Low hurdles** need a short hop; **high walls** need a held jump; **pits** need a precise takeoff; **double hurdles** need rhythm; **rolling rocks** scroll faster; **sand zones** slow you down.
- **Full load**: while your back is full of jars, consecutive jumps trail golden arcs behind you (score bonus).
- Cross three hand-painted scene packs — Silk Dawn (sand & gold), Oasis Noon (emerald), Star Night (blue & violet) — across 12 legs of rotating obstacles, with speed rising the further you go.

你是一头驮满陶罐的小驴，沿丝绸之路自动奔跑。每一只陶罐都是你的命——撞一下掉一罐，罐子掉光商队就散架。**点击/空格**小跳，**长按**高跳过墙，**K 键**丢一罐换一次超高跳。矮栏短跳、高墙长按、坑洞要踩准起跳点、连环栏要踩节拍、滚石更快、风沙区减速。满载时连跳，身后陶罐会划出金色弧线。跨越丝路晨（沙金）、绿洲午（翠绿）、星夜（蓝紫）三大场景、十二段障碍轮换，越跑越快，看你能把商队赶到多远。
