CATEGORY

TAGS

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Pull the sluice gates in the right order to pour golden honey into bottles and keep bubbles out.

LONG DESCRIPTION
PIN & POUR is a free pin-pull puzzle game set in a cozy honey workshop.

Tap the wooden sluice gates to release the golden honey and let gravity pour it down the filling table. Every drop must land in a glass bottle — but watch out for the bubbles: one bubble slipping into a bottle pollutes the whole batch, and one drop of honey running down the wrong drain wastes it. Pull the gates in the right order and the amber light flashes as each jar fills.

12 handcrafted levels across three worlds: the Morning Apiary, the Noon Workshop and the Dusk Cellar. Honeycomb blocks melt away under flowing honey, fine screens let bubbles through but stop the thick honey, and later levels stack traps and twin flows that must be timed just right.

Three stars per level: finish the pour, keep every bubble out, and pull no more gates than needed. Easy to learn in ten seconds, tricky to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.

《蜜糖分装》是一款免费的抽闸益智游戏，舞台在一间温馨的蜂蜜工坊分装台。

点击木闸板，放出金色蜂蜜，让重力把它一路灌下分装台。每一滴都必须落进玻璃瓶——但要当心气泡：一颗气泡掉进瓶子就会污染整批蜂蜜，一滴好蜜流进错误的排口也会白白浪费。按正确顺序抽闸，琥珀色的光就会在每一瓶装满时亮起。

12 个手工关卡横跨三大场景：晨间蜂场、午后工坊、夜幕蜜窖。蜂巢块会被流过的蜂蜜冲开，细密的滤网只放气泡通过、却挡得住粘稠的蜂蜜，后面的关卡还会叠上时间差陷阱与双股汇流，节奏必须恰到好处。

每关三星：完成分装、不让任何气泡混入、抽闸步数不超过最少步数。十秒上手，越玩越烧脑。鼠标、触屏、键盘全支持，桌面与手机均可游玩。无需下载，浏览器免费畅玩。
