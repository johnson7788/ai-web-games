SHORT DESCRIPTION
Guard the herb garden as a bamboo swordsman — move, auto-slash foraging beasts, and forge your Sword Intent.

LONG DESCRIPTION
BLADE GARDEN is a free top-down survivor action game about a bamboo swordsman guarding a medicine garden from foraging beasts.

Move with WASD or a virtual joystick and your blade strikes the nearest beast on its own. Slay boars, crows, mandrills and herb thieves, absorb their Qi motes, and pick one of three Sword Intents each time you level up — sharpen your blade, unleash bamboo leaf darts, spinning whirl slashes, thunder bamboo, or root tangle. When every Sword Intent is maxed, a storm of bamboo leaves swirls around you.

12 timed levels across three seasons: the Spring Plot, the Summer Marsh and the Autumn Cliff. Each level adds a new threat — elite beasts, the Medicine Cauldron boss, poison mires, narrow passes, and the final Mandrill King. Survive the clock and hit the slay quota to clear each stage.

Easy to learn in ten seconds, deep enough to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.
