# PLUMBER RUN

Run, jump, and sling a boomerang wrench through the steam city as wrench master **Aban**. Hit question blocks to grow from small to big to fire form, stomp pipe monsters, grab steam canisters to charge your tank, and leap to the flagpole to fix the city — 12 levels, 3 stars each, 3 hand-painted scene packs.

**How to play**
- Move: ← → or A / D
- Jump (hold for a higher jump): Space or ↑ / W
- Throw the boomerang wrench: J (it hits enemies on the way out *and* on the way back)
- Steam burst (full tank = one super jump + 1.5s hover): K or Shift

Every level rotates a new twist: pits, moving platforms, pipe gates, water currents, a pitch-dark tunnel where only your wrench casts light, spikes, and pressure locks. The wrench is short-handed — once you throw it, you can't throw again until it returns — and a full steam tank can only be spent once. Choose: risk the coins or race to the flag? Throw the wrench now, or keep it for the next monster? Spend your steam on a high jump, or save it for combat?
