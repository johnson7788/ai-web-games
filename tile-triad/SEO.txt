LONG DESCRIPTION
TILE TRIAD is a free layered tile-matching puzzle game set in a cozy herbal market.

Tap glowing top cards to move them into a seven-slot tray, and match three of the same good — spice jars, dried flowers, fruit baskets, lanterns and more — to clear them in a puff of spice dust while the shopkeeper cat cheers you on. Clearing the top card uncovers the one beneath it, so every pick is a gamble: grab a match now, or dig deeper and risk flooding your tray.

12 handcrafted levels across three markets: Dawn Market, Afternoon Market and the Night Lantern Fair. The tray shrinks from 7 slots to 6, the stacks grow from 2 layers to 5, and 6 goods expand to 12 as cap layers, twin towers, fake-locked tiles, timed stalls and prop levels keep every run fresh. Undo and shuffle props give you a lifeline when the stall gets out of hand.

Every level is guaranteed solvable without props. Three stars per level reward clean, prop-free clears. Easy to learn in ten seconds, tricky to master. Works with mouse, touch and keyboard on desktop and mobile. No download, play free in your browser.
