Title: RED FORTRESS — Free Top-Down Jeep Shooter Game

Description:
RED FORTRESS is a free top-down vertical-scrolling vehicle shooter in your browser.
Drive the last jeep into the enemy fortress, tear through the defense with a machine gun
and grenades, and ferry a chain of rescued POWs back to the helipad — the more you save,
the harder you hit.

- 4 handcrafted themed stages: Jungle Dawn, River Crossing, Desert Outpost, Red Fortress
- Signature mechanic: POWs trail behind your jeep; deliver 8 in one stage to call a gunship airstrike
- Grenades blast sandbag walls and wipe enemy bullets; the terrain stays scarred
- 7 enemy types and 2 bosses: a heavy tank and the Fortress Core (4 cannons → shield down → core)
- Choose 1 of 3 upgrades between stages, 8 stackable mods: AP rounds, twin gun, HE grenade, turbo…
- 3 lives, English and Chinese, keyboard/mouse and mobile touch
- Plays instantly in the browser — no download, no account, works offline
