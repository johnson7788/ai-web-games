# PENTA CARDS

**PENTA CARDS** is a cosmic 3-player card game inspired by the classic "Five Star" table game. You face two AI opponents across a 12-round rank climb through three themed tables — the blue-gold Observatory, the warm Tea House, and the cyan-purple Aurora.

## How to play

- **Goal**: form a winning hand of 4 melds (triplets or runs) plus one pair, or 7 pairs.
- **Draw and discard**: on your turn, draw a tile, then click a tile in your hand to discard it.
- **Claim**: when an opponent discards, you may **Pong** (two of a kind), **Kong** (three/four of a kind), or **Hu** (win) with the matching tile.
- **Stargaze** (2×/round): peek the top 3 tiles of the wall.
- **Swap** (1×/round): discard one tile blind and draw a replacement — but your opponents see what you dropped.
- **Five Star**: winning with the 5 of Star or Orbit sandwiched between the 4 and the 6 earns a bonus fan.
- Score is `2^fan × bet`; winning by self-draw collects from both rivals, winning on a discard collects from the discarder.

Controls: click tiles and buttons; keyboard `Esc` pauses, `R` continues after a round, `Enter` starts from the title.

**PENTA CARDS** 是一款宇宙主题的三人卡牌游戏，灵感来自经典「卡五星」牌局。你与两名 AI 对手在 12 局段位赛中对战，依次经过观星台（蓝金）、茶室（暖褐）、极光（青紫）三张牌桌。

**玩法**：凑成 4 副牌（刻子或顺子）+ 1 对，或七对即可胡牌。回合内摸牌后点手牌弃牌；对手弃牌时可碰、杠或胡。每局可**观星** 2 次（看牌堆顶 3 张）与**换星** 1 次（弃 1 换 1，对手可见）。以「星 5 或轨 5」夹在 4、6 之间胡牌即触发招牌「卡五星」加番。分数为 `2^番 × 底注`，自摸收两家、点炮收放炮者。
