# BUBBLE REEF

BUBBLE REEF is a glowing jellyfish bubble shooter set across three hand-crafted reef scenes — the bright Shallow Reef, the bioluminescent Night Reef, and the rust-green Sunken Wreck. You control a tide cannon at the bottom of the sea. Aim and fire luminous jellyfish bubbles onto a hexagonal grid, match 3 or more of the same color to release them back into the ocean, and watch unsupported clusters break away into a swarm of drifting jellyfish for bonus points.

Every level is a race against the sinking ceiling: the top of the field descends one row every few shots, and if any bubble touches the bottom line, the tide wins. Chain combos to earn a wildcard bubble (matches any color), and use your once-per-level tide preview to trace the full bouncing trajectory before you commit. Across 12 levels you'll face rock obstacles that can only be cleared by collapse, drifting currents that curve your shots, double ceilings, five-color grids, and gold bubbles you must clear to finish.

## How to play

- **Move** the mouse / drag your finger to aim the cannon.
- **Click / release** to fire the current bubble.
- **Match 3** same-colored bubbles to clear them; unsupported clusters fall for bonus points.
- **W** or tap the wildcard button to arm a rainbow bubble (costs 3 combo).
- **P** or tap the preview button to reveal the full trajectory (once per level).
- **Esc** to pause · **R** to restart after a run.
