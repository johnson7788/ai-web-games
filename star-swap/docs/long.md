# STAR SWAP — Deep-Space Observatory Match-3

In the quiet dark of a deep-space observatory, the telescope is full of stardust. Swap two neighbouring stars to line up three or more of one colour and they dissolve into light. Clear four in a row to forge a **row-blast** or **column-blast** special star. Clear five to trigger a **supernova** — a full-screen white flash that annihilates every star of one colour.

Every level has a target score and a limited number of moves. Reach the target before your moves run out to advance across **12 sectors** and three observatories: the purple-blue **Milky River**, the cyan-white **Orion**, and the gold-orange **South Gate**. Along the way new rules appear: **dark-matter cells** that block the board until a neighbouring special star blows them open, **six-colour boards** that are harder to match, and one-turn **gravity reversal** that refills stardust from the bottom.

Your signature tool is the **gravity lens** — three charges per level that let you swap two stars from across the board, bypassing the normal "neighbours only" rule. Spend a lens on a critical move, or save it for the late game.

**Controls**
- Click or tap a star, then tap a neighbour to swap them.
- Tap the lens button (bottom-right) to enter lens mode, then tap any two stars to swap them across the board.
- Esc / P to pause. R to retry after a loss. Enter to start.

## 中文说明

在深空观测站的望远镜里，星屑正等着你去交换。点击相邻两颗星屑交换位置，同色连成三颗即可消除；四连可锻造**行爆**或**列爆**特殊星；五连触发**超新星**——全屏白闪、同色星屑全部湮灭。

每关有目标分和有限步数：在步数耗尽前达标即可推进，共 **12 关**、三个星区——紫蓝的**河内（天河）**、青白的**猎户**、金橙的**南门**。关卡中会轮换新机制：**暗物质格**占位不消除（靠相邻特殊星爆开）、**六色关**更难成连、**重力反转**让星屑从底部回落。

本作招牌道具是**引力透镜**——每关 3 次，可放大 2×2 区域做「隔空换位」，打破只能相邻交换的限制。用在关键步还是留到后期，全看你的取舍。

**操作**：点击/点触一颗星再点相邻星交换；点右下角透镜进入透镜模式后可点任意两颗星隔空换位；Esc/P 暂停，失败后按 R 重来，回车开始。
